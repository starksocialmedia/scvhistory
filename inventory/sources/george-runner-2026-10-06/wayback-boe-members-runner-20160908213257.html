Welcome

I am thankful and honored to represent more than nine million Californians as an elected member of the State Board of Equalization.

As a Board member, my number one priority is to advocate for taxpayers. Hardworking Californians deserve to have someone working on their behalf in support of fair tax policies that will create jobs and grow our economy.

I hope my website serves as a useful tool. Here you will find taxpayer resources, learn my opinions on current issues, and can leave comments and feedback. Should you need assistance with tax-related problems or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact me.

GEORGE RUNNER
First District
