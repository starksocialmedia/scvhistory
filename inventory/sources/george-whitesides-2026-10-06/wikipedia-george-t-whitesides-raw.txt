'''George Thomas Whitesides''' (born March 3, 1974)<ref name=":1" /> is an American politician and businessman who has served as the [[United States House of Representatives|U.S. representative]] for [[California's 27th congressional district]] since 2025. He was previously chief of staff at [[NASA]] and CEO of [[Virgin Galactic]].<ref name="Fast Company2">{{Cite web |last=Dugdale |first=Addy |date=May 29, 2010 |title=Crib Sheet: George T. Whitesides, Top Dog (in Space) at Virgin Galactic |url=https://www.fastcompany.com/1650162/crib-sheet-george-t-whitesides-top-dog-space-virgin-galactic |access-date=April 7, 2023 |website=[[Fast Company]] |language=en-US}}</ref> He is a member of the [[Democratic Party (United States)|Democratic Party]].

Whitesides' district covers northern [[Los Angeles County, California|Los Angeles County]], and includes [[Santa Clarita, California|Santa Clarita]], [[Palmdale, California|Palmdale]], [[Lancaster, California|Lancaster]], and parts of the northwestern [[San Fernando Valley]].

==Early life and education==
Whitesides was born on March 3, 1974, in [[Massachusetts]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Whitesides, George |url=https://bioguide.congress.gov/search/bio/W000830 |access-date=2025-03-04 |website=[[Biographical Directory of the United States Congress]]}}</ref> He was raised in [[Newton, Massachusetts|Newton]] with his brother.<ref name="Move2007">{{cite journal |last1=Arnaud |first1=Celia Henry |date=March 26, 2007 |title=Always On The Move |url=https://pubs.acs.org/cen/coverstory/85/8513cover2.html |journal=Chemical & Engineering News |volume=85 |issue=13 |pages=18–25 |access-date=January 21, 2015}}</ref><ref name=":1">{{Cite web |title=Rep. George Whitesides - D California, 27th, In Office - Biography {{!}} LegiStorm |url=https://www.legistorm.com/person/bio/458450/George_Thomas_Whitesides.html |access-date=2025-03-04 |website=www.legistorm.com |language=en}}</ref> His great-grandfather, [[James Breasted]], was an [[egyptologist]] who coined the term "[[Fertile Crescent|fertile crescent]]".<ref>{{Cite book |last=Whitesides |first=Barbara B. |url=https://books.google.com/books?id=FOPxwAEACAAJ |title=Sugar Comes from Arabic: A Beginner's Guide to Arabic Letters and Words |date=2009 |publisher=Interlink Books |isbn=978-1-56656-757-2 |language=en}}</ref><ref name="abt">{{cite book|last=Abt|first=Jeffrey|year=2011|url=https://books.google.com/books?id=YEc0bc93LwYC|title=American Egyptologist: the life of James Henry Breasted and the creation of his Oriental Institute|location=Chicago|publisher=University of Chicago Press|isbn=978-0-226-00110-4|pages=193–194, 436}}</ref> While growing up, Whitesides played ice hockey<ref name="Move2007" /> and attended [[Newton North High School]], where he graduated in 1992.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Abrams |first=Meredith |date=March 25, 2011 |title=Virgin Galactic CEO presents information on space tourism |url=https://issuu.com/newtonite/docs/v90i04master |access-date=April 7, 2023 |website=The Newtonite |pages=6–7 |language=en |via=[[Issuu]]}}</ref>

Whitesides went on to attend [[Princeton University]], where he studied at the [[Woodrow Wilson School of Public and International Affairs]], earning his bachelor's degree in 1996.<ref name="Albuquerque Journal 2023-04-07">{{Cite news |last=Walz |first=Kent |date=May 18, 2019 |title=Face to Face with George T. Whitesides: Reaching for the stars |url=https://www.abqjournal.com/1317540/face-to-face-with-george-t-whitesides-reaching-for-the-stars-ex-virgin-galactic-ceo-says-nmbased-flights-would-be-a-historic-first-step.html |access-date=April 7, 2023 |website=[[Albuquerque Journal]] |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Daugherty |first=Elisabeth H. |date=July 20, 2021 |title=July 20: George Whitesides '96 Brings People With Disabilities Into Space |url=https://paw.princeton.edu/article/july-20-george-whitesides-96-brings-people-disabilities-space |access-date=April 7, 2023 |website=[[Princeton Alumni Weekly]] |publisher=[[Princeton University]] Press |language=en-US}}</ref> After graduating, he served for four years on Princeton's [[Board of Trustees]].<ref name="NASA bio2">{{Cite web |date=May 1, 2010 |title=George T. Whitesides, NASA's Chief of Staff |url=https://www.nasa.gov/about/highlights/whitesides_bio.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230425034506/https://www.nasa.gov/about/highlights/whitesides_bio.html |archive-date=April 25, 2023 |access-date=April 7, 2023 |publisher=[[NASA]] |language=en}}</ref> He later earned an [[Master of Philosophy|MPhil]] in [[geographic information system]]s and [[remote sensing]] from [[King's College, Cambridge]],<ref name="Fast Company2"/> and was a [[Fulbright Scholar]] in [[Tunisia]].<ref name="Albuquerque Journal 2023-04-07" /><ref name="Arab News 2018-12-272">{{Cite news |last=Kane |first=Frank |date=December 27, 2018 |title=INTERVIEW: 'I have always dreamed of going into space' — George Whitesides, CEO Virgin Galactic |url=https://www.arabnews.com/node/1426316/business-economy |access-date=April 7, 2023 |website=[[Arab News]] |language=en-US}}</ref>

==Space career==
[[File:Around_the_World_and_Beyond_-_George_Whitesides_(cropped2).png|thumb|Whitesides speaking at the [[World Economic Forum]], 2017]]
Whitesides began his career in the space sector, where he served as executive director of the [[National Space Society]] from 2004 to 2008.<ref>{{cite web |date=May 16, 2006 |title=George Whitesides |url=http://www.nss.org/about/bios/whitesides.html |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080511212907/http://www.nss.org/about/bios/whitesides.html |archive-date=May 11, 2008 |accessdate=February 4, 2007 |publisher=National Space Society}}</ref> He also co-founded [[Yuri's Night]], an annual celebration of [[human spaceflight]], alongside his wife [[Loretta Hidalgo Whitesides]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Hidalgo-Whitesides |first=Loretta |author2=Whitesides, G. |title=About : what is yuri's night? |url=http://www.yurisnight.net/2007/page.php?p=http://www.yurisnight.net/2007/content/2_1_about.php |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20080413034711/http://www.yurisnight.net/2007/page.php?p=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.yurisnight.net%2F2007%2Fcontent%2F2_1_about.php |archive-date=April 13, 2008 |accessdate=February 4, 2007 |publisher=Yuri's Night}}</ref><ref name="Albuquerque Journal 2023-04-07" /> During this period, he was named senior advisor to [[Virgin Galactic]], [[Richard Branson]]'s space tourism company.<ref>{{cite web |last=Galactic |first=Virgin |date=June 21, 2007 |title=Virgin Galactic Appoints George Whitesides as Senior Advisor |url=http://forum.nasaspaceflight.com/forums/thread-view.asp?tid=8503&posts=3&start=1 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://archive.today/20120713223725/http://forum.nasaspaceflight.com/forums/thread-view.asp?tid=8503&posts=3&start=1 |archive-date=July 13, 2012 |accessdate=March 22, 2008 |publisher=NASASpaceflight}}</ref>

Whitesides worked on the 2008 [[Presidential transition of Barack Obama|presidential transition]] of [[Barack Obama]].<ref>{{cite web |title=George Whitesides |url=https://spaceforhumanity.org/about/team/george-whitesides |accessdate=April 24, 2023 |website=[[Space For Humanity]]}}</ref> After Obama took office, he was appointed chief of staff of [[National Aeronautics and Space Administration|NASA]],<ref name="Albuquerque Journal 2023-04-07" /> where he served until 2010 under Administrator [[Charles Bolden]].<ref name="NASA bio2"/><ref name="Fast Company2"/> Upon his departure, he was awarded the [[NASA Distinguished Service Medal]], the agency's highest award.<ref name="Arab News 2018-12-272"/><ref>{{cite web |date=c. 2015 |title=Historical Awards Recipient List |url=https://searchpub.nssc.nasa.gov/servlet/sm.web.Fetch/Agency_Awards_Historical_Recipient_List.pdf?rhid=1000&did=2120817&type=released |accessdate=April 24, 2023 |publisher=[[NASA]] |page=233}}</ref>

After leaving NASA, Whitesides became Virgin Galactic's first [[CEO]] and led the company for a decade.<ref name="Albuquerque Journal 2023-04-07" /> He oversaw the development of commercial [[spaceflight]] vehicles and managed operations at the [[Mojave Air and Space Port]].<ref name=":0">{{cite web |date=May 17, 2010 |title=Virgin Galactic Snags NASA Chief of Staff as CEO |url=http://www.socaltech.com/virgin_galactic_snags_nasa_chief_of_staff_as_ceo/s-0028664.html |accessdate=May 17, 2010 |website=socalTECH.com}}</ref> During this time, he also chaired the Reusable Launch Vehicle Working Group for [[COMSTAC]], the advisory committee for the [[FAA]]'s Commercial Space Transportation Division.<ref name="Albuquerque Journal 2023-04-07" /> Additionally, he served as a board member for [[Astronomers Without Borders]] and the Space Generation Foundation.<ref name="NASA bio2"/><ref name="Fast Company2"/> He announced in 2021 his departure as CEO of Virgin Galactic while remaining in an advisory role with the company.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Foust |first=Jeff |date=March 5, 2021 |title=Whitesides steps down from Virgin Galactic |url=https://spacenews.com/whitesides-steps-down-from-virgin-galactic/ |access-date=March 5, 2021 |work=[[Space News]]}}</ref>

In December 2022, Whitesides co-sponsored a research parabolic flight with AstroAccess.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Howell |first=Elizabeth |date=December 20, 2022 |title=Zero-G flight for disability ambassadors shows space is accessible for all |url=https://www.space.com/disability-ambassadors-astroaccess-zero-g-science |access-date=April 7, 2023 |website=[[Space.com]] |language=en-US}}</ref>

==U.S. House of Representatives==
===2024 election===
{{See also|2024 United States House of Representatives elections in California#District 27|label 1=2024 California's 27th congressional district election}}
[[File:California's_27th_congressional_district_(since_2023)_(new_version).svg|thumb|[[California's 27th congressional district|California's 27th district]] since 2023 ]]
On February 22, 2023, Whitesides announced he was running for [[California's 27th congressional district]] in the [[2024 United States House of Representatives elections in California|2024 election]] as a [[Democratic Party (United States)|Democrat]], challenging incumbent Republican congressman [[Mike Garcia (politician)|Mike Garcia]].<ref name="2024run">{{Cite news |date=February 22, 2023 |title=Former Virgin Galactic CEO enters US House race north of LA |url=https://apnews.com/article/politics-united-states-government-house-of-representatives-2022-midterm-elections-los-angeles-b078cdfbd629cc971a5c0e7710c32d03 |access-date=April 17, 2023 |work=[[Associated Press News]] |language=en-US}}</ref> As a first-time candidate without a legislative voting record, Republican opposition focused on the [[Progressivism in the United States|progressive]] groups and candidates that contributed to his campaign, including [[Equality California]], which co-sponsored the controversial 2020 California Senate Bill 145 regarding judicial discretion in sex offender registration.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Branson-Potts |first1=Hailey |date=23 October 2024 |title=A theme in GOP attack ads in California House races: Linking Democrats to pedophiles over support for LGBTQ group |url=https://www.latimes.com/california/story/2024-10-23/la-me-republican-pedophile-attack-ad |access-date=3 March 2025 |work=Los Angeles Times}}</ref>

Whitesides narrowly defeated Garcia in the general election on November 5, 2024, by around 8,000 votes.<ref>{{cite news |last1=Branson-Potts |first1=Hailey |date=November 11, 2024 |title=Whitesides captures L.A. County congressional seat in a major victory for Democrats |url=https://www.latimes.com/california/story/2024-11-11/la-me-congress-california-27-garcia-whitesides |access-date=November 11, 2024 |work=Los Angeles Times}}</ref>

=== Tenure ===
Whitesides was sworn into office on January 3, 2025.<ref name=":02">{{Cite web |date= |title=George Whitesides |url=https://clerk.house.gov/members/W000830 |archive-url= |archive-date= |access-date=2025-05-27 |website=Office of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives}}</ref> During his first term, he was appointed to the [[House Armed Services Committee]] and the [[United States House Committee on Science, Space, and Technology|Science, Space, and Technology Committee]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Fernholz |first=Tim |date=2025-05-23 |title=Payload Talks NASA Budget With Rep. George Whitesides |url=https://payloadspace.com/payload-talks-nasa-budget-with-rep-george-whitesides/ |access-date=2025-05-28 |website=Payload |language=en-US}}</ref> In March 2025, he co-sponsored the bipartisan DOE and NASA Interagency Research Coordination Act, a bill introduced by [[Nick Begich III|Nick Begich]] (R-AK), which passed the House and formalized collaboration between the [[United States Department of Energy|Department of Energy]] and NASA to advance space exploration and energy innovation.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Downing |first=Suzanne |date=2025-03-25 |title=Congressman Begich's third bill passes House |url=https://mustreadalaska.com/congressman-begichs-third-bill-passes-house/ |access-date=2025-05-28 |website=Must Read Alaska |language=en-US}}</ref>

During his first week in office, Whitesides returned to [[Los Angeles]] to assist with wildfire response efforts, helping coordinate on-the-ground outreach following the [[Hurst Fire]].<ref>{{Cite news |last=Pinho |first=Faith |date=January 30, 2025 |title=These House newcomers came to the Capitol with big plans. Then the wildfires hit |url=https://www.latimes.com/politics/story/2025-01-30/california-house-newcomers-wildfires |work=Los Angeles Times}}</ref> In response to the broader [[January 2025 Southern California wildfires|2025 Southern California wildfires]], Whitesides – who had co-founded a wildfire prevention organization and emphasized urban wildfire risks during his campaign – co-sponsored the bipartisan Fix Our Forests Act, which passed the House and aimed to improve [[forest management]] by easing environmental regulations and improving coordination of science-based fire prevention efforts across government agencies.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Pinho |first=Faith |date=January 16, 2025 |title=In the wake of L.A.-area fires, Congress will consider new measures. Here are some of them |url=https://www.latimes.com/politics/story/2025-01-16/palisades-eaton-altadena-los-angeles-wildfires-federal-legislation |work=Los Angeles Times}}</ref>
