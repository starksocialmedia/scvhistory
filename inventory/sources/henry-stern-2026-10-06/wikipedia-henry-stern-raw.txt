'''Henry Isaac Stern''' (born April 12, 1982) is an American politician and member of the [[California State Senate]]. He is a [[California Democratic Party|Democrat]] representing the [[California's 27th State Senate district|27th district]], encompassing parts of [[Los Angeles County, California|Los Angeles]] and [[Ventura County, California|Ventura]] counties. He was elected in November 2016.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.ourcampaigns.com/RaceDetail.html?RaceID=814747|title=2016 CA State Senate 27|website=Our Campaigns}}</ref> Prior to being elected to the State Senate, he was an environmental attorney and senior advisor to his predecessor [[Fran Pavley]]. He is the first [[Millennials|millennial]] elected to the California State Senate. He was also an American law lecturer at the [[University of California, Los Angeles]] and [[University of California, Berkeley|UC Berkeley]].<ref>{{cite web|url= https://sd27.senate.ca.gov/biography|title=Biography Page|work=California State Senate|date=November 21, 2016 }}</ref>

Stern has served as counsel to Representative [[Henry Waxman]] on the [[United States House Committee on Energy and Commerce|House Energy & Commerce Committee]] to help construct clean energy projects for businesses. He has also taught civics, founded a tech incubator, and advocated for juvenile justice.

== Early life and education ==
Henry Isaac Stern was born to a [[American Jews|Jewish]] family<ref>{{Cite web|last=Arom|first=Eitan|title=Jewish state legislators ready to make an impact |publisher=[[The Jewish Journal of Greater Los Angeles|Jewish Journal]]|date=January 6, 2017|url=http://jewishjournal.com/news/nation/213428/}}</ref> in [[Malibu, California|Malibu]], [[California]] on April 12, 1982. His father is actor [[Daniel Stern (actor)|Daniel Stern]], who is best known for his role as Marv Murchins in the films ''[[Home Alone]]'' and ''[[Home Alone 2: Lost in New York]]''.

A former high school and collegiate water polo player, Stern graduated from [[Harvard University]].<ref>{{cite web|last=Sabala |first=Michael C. |url=http://www.thecrimson.com/article/2000/10/16/harvard-crushes-yale-and-dartmouth-pthe/?page=2 |title=Harvard Crushes Yale and Dartmouth |publisher=The Harvard Crimson |date=October 16, 2000 |access-date=April 30, 2017}}</ref> He went on to graduate from the [[UC Berkeley School of Law]], before becoming an environmental attorney.

== California State Senate (2016–) ==
=== Election ===
Henry Stern represented the Democratic Party in the 2016 race for California's 27th State Senate district against Republican Steve Fazio.<ref>{{Cite news|url=http://www.dailynews.com/government-and-politics/20160607/election-2016-steve-fazio-and-henry-stern-advance-in-senate-district-27-race|title=Election 2016: Steve Fazio and Henry Stern advance in Senate District 27 race|access-date=November 9, 2016}}</ref> On November 8, 2016, he was elected to the seat.<ref>{{Cite news|url=https://www.nytimes.com/elections/results/california-state-senate-district-27|title=California 27th District State Senate Results: Henry Stern Wins|access-date=November 9, 2016}}</ref>

Stern ran on a platform of five main issues in the 2016 elections. These included standing up to oil and gas companies; fighting to create incentives for companies to create "clean transportation and renewable energy infrastructure;" improving the economy with small businesses, [[clean technology]], good-paying jobs and job training; supporting education by securing funding; and creating safer communities by providing funding to local governments.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.henrysternforsenate.com/district-issues/|title=Fighting For Better Society – District Issues |date=September 17, 2024 }}</ref>

During the 2016 election, Stern was endorsed by Senator Fran Pavley, the Sierra Club, the California League of Conservation Voters, teachers and school board members from Los Angeles and Ventura county school districts, the Association of Los Angeles County Sheriffs, and the California Association of Highway Patrolmen.<ref name="supp" /> He was endorsed by elected officials such as President Barack Obama and Governor Jerry Brown, among many more. Publications such as the Ventura County Star and Ventura County Signal endorsed him as well.<ref name="supp">{{Cite web|url=https://www.henrysternforsenate.com/henrys-supporters/|title=Fighting For Better Society – Henry's Supporters |date=September 17, 2024 }}</ref>

== Los Angeles County Board of Supervisors election ==
Stern announced his candidacy for the District 3 seat on the [[Los Angeles County Board of Supervisors]] in 2022.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.smdp.com/kuehl-decries-new-third-district-boundaries/211531|title=Kuehl decries new third district boundaries|first=Emily|last=Sawicki|date=December 17, 2021|website=Santa Monica Daily Press}}</ref> On June 22, Stern conceded the race after receiving 24% of the vote in the June 7 primary.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Steepleton |first1=Scott |title=Stern concedes L.A. County supervisor race |url=https://www.theacorn.com/articles/stern-concedes-l-a-county-supervisor-race/ |website=www.theacorn.com |date=June 30, 2022 |publisher=The Acorn |access-date=16 November 2022}}</ref>

== In popular culture ==
Henry's father, Daniel Stern, performed the narrative voiceover of adult Kevin Arnold for the hit television series ''[[The Wonder Years]]'' for six seasons. At the end of the show's [[series finale]], Henry delivered the next-to-last line of the entire series when he called to his father—in voiceover—"Hey Dad, wanna play catch?" to which his father replies "I'll be right there."<ref>{{cite news|last=Kahn|first=Debra|title=Former Waxman aide vying to succeed Calif. climate luminary|date=May 2, 2016|newspaper=[[E&E News]]|access-date=November 20, 2025|url=https://www.eenews.net/articles/former-waxman-aide-vying-to-succeed-calif-climate-luminary|quote=Henry Stern himself had a bit part in "The Wonder Years," doing a voice-over in the last episode as the son of the main character, Kevin Arnold.}}</ref>
