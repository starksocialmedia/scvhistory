'''Audra Strickland''' (born July 10, 1974) is an American politician who served as a member of the [[California State Assembly]] for the 37th district from December 6, 2004 to November 30, 2010.<ref>{{cite web|title=Join California - Audra Strickland|url=http://www.joincalifornia.com/candidate/6164|website=joincalifornia.com}}</ref><ref>https://www.ourcampaigns.com/CandidateDetail.html?CandidateID=35489</ref> She succeeded her then-husband, [[Tony Strickland]], who was term-limited. Before serving in the Assembly, she was a junior high school teacher for a year at Temple Christian School in [[Ventura, California]].

==Education==
While earning her [[Bachelor of Arts]] degree in [[political science]], Strickland served as president of the Ronald Reagan Republican Club at the [[University of California, Irvine]]. She was a [[Jesse M. Unruh Assembly Fellowship|California State Assembly Fellow]] for Assemblyman [[Bill Campbell (California politician)|Bill Campbell]]. Also, She worked for former [[Speaker of the California State Assembly|Assembly Speaker]] [[Curt Pringle]] and Assemblyman [[Chuck Poochigian]].

Audra Strickland filed to run for Secretary of State in 2010.  Strickland ended her quest for Secretary of State and announced her intention to run for Ventura County Treasurer Tax Collector. ==Personal==
Audra lives in [[Moorpark, California]] with her two children.  Her former husband, [[Tony Strickland]], became Mayor of [[Huntington Beach, California]], in December 2022.
