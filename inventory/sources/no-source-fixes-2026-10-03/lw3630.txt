SCVHistory.com LW3630 | Film-Arts | Lobby Card: Dale Robertson, Rodolfo Acosta in ''City of Bad Men'' (1953). > RODOLFO ACOSTA "City of Bad Men" (1953) With Dale Robertson and Rodolfo Acosta Click image to enlarge | Download archival scan CITY OF BAD MEN (1953) Lobby Card No. 7 Two honorees on the Downtown Newhall Walk of Western Stars — Dale Robertson (1988/89), center-left, and Rodolfo Acosta (2013), center-right — are featured on this lobby card advertising 1953's "City of Bad Men" from Twentieth Century-Fox. Partially filmed at Vasquez Rocks , "City of Bad Men" also features one more "Walk" honoree — Lloyd Bridges (2010). Rounding out the credited cast are Jeanne Crain, Richard Boone, Carole Mathews, Carl Betz, Whitfield Connor, Hugh Sanders and Pascual García Peña.
