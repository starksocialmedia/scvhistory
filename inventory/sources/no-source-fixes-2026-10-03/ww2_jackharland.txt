March 31, 1944 --> Home of Record: Date of Birth: Service: Army of the United States Rank: Second Lieutenant ID No: 0-704928 Specialty: Pilot Length of Service: Unit: Start Tour: Incident Date: March 31, 1944 Casualty Date: March 31, 1944 Age at Loss: 28 Location: Gate, Okla. Remains: Narrative: 2LT Jack Harland at age 28 was the copilot of a B-24 that collided in midair with another B-24 and crashed near Gate, Okla., during a training exercise on March 31, 1944.
