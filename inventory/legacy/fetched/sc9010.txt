 Carl Boyer III 
 Santa Clarita City Council 
Click image to enlarge
 Carl Boyer III (b. September 22, 1937). Resident of Newhall.
Santa Clarita City Council member, 1987-1998. Mayor in 1991 and 1996.
 [City of Santa Clarita 1998] —
 Career Activities: 
 Carl Boyer was elected to the first City Council of Santa Clarita in 1987, and has since been re-elected twice.
 In 1991 he served as the Mayor of the City of Santa Clarita. A highlight during his tenure as Mayor was exercising with Arnold Schwarzenegger.
 Community affiliations: 
 Carl Boyer has always been a community activist. He has worked with several organizations including the Canyon County Formation Committee for five years and then the City Formation Committee. 
 Served as an elected official on the Community College Board from 1973 to 1981 . 
 Served on the Castaic Lake Water Agency from 1982 to 1984. 
 Treasurer of Healing the Children California. 
 Education: 
 Earned a B.A. degree in History from Trinity University, 1959. 
 Earned a Masters degree in Education from the University of Cincinnati, 1962. 
 Studied at Edinburgh University in Scotland, 1956-57. 
 Personal: 
 Carl Boyer has been a resident of the Santa Clarita Valley for almost 30 years. He is married to his wife, Chris, and they have three daughters and a granddaughter. Carl has written many books on family history.
