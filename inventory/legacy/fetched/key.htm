Photographs are presented in a high-resolution JPEG format and have been enhanced for maximum clarity and detail. In most cases, the image obtained through "click to enlarge" or "download original/archival scan" is the highest and best form in which the image exists and is delivered in extremely high resolution (usually 9600 dpi). Most images have no print size. If you're printing one, you need to give it a print size in Photoshop (or similar) and reduce the resolution (dpi). There is no access to physical original prints.
