Rudy Alexander Acosta U.S. Army | d. March 19, 2011



Home of Record: Canyon Country Date of Birth: May 2, 1991 High School: Santa Clarita Christian School

Service: Army of the United States Rank: Specialist 4th Class ID No:  Specialty: Medic Length of Service:  Unit: 4th Squadron, 2nd Stryker Cavalry Regiment Start Tour: Based: Vilseck, Germany Supporting: Operation Enduring Freedom

Incident Date: March 19, 2011 Casualty Date: March 19, 2011 Age at Loss: 20 Location: Kandahar province, Afghanistan Remains: Eternal Valley Cemetery, Newhall

Narrative: SP4 Rudy A. Acosta of Canyon Country served as an Army Medic with the 2nd Stryker Calvary Regiment in Kandahar, Afghanistan where he was fatally shot at age 20 by a rogue Afghani security soldier on March 19, 2011. SP4 Rudy Acosta was awarded the Bronze Star and Purple Heart among numerous other citations. His rank was elevated posthumously to SP4 from PFC. SP4 Acosta is buried at Eternal Valley Cemetery in Newhall, Calif.
