SB634 (Wilk, 2017): SCV Water Agency Enabling Legislation. Approved by Gov.Jerry Brown, October 15, 2017.


[ Open full-screen (Book View) ]   [ Open original .pdf ]




Senate Bill 634 (Wilk, 2017) repeals the Castaic Lake Water Agency law (Section 57114.5 of the Government Code) and establishes the Santa Clarita Valley Water Agency.
