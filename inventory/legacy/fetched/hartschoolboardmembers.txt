Governing Board Members William S. Hart Union High School District 1945 to Date.



March 9, 1945 (First Board Elected)
Thomas M. Frew Jr., Newhall School District
S.S. Donaldson, Newhall School District
Mary P. Bonelli, Saugus School District
Mildred Gilmour, Castaic School District
Charles Brown, Mint Canyon and Sulphur Springs School Districts
Note: The Newhall, Saugus, Castaic, Mint Canyon and Sulphur Springs districts "united" to form the Santa Clarita "Union" High School District, which later changed its name to Hart. All five of the new trustees were current trustees in their respective elementary districts except for Frew, who had retired from the Newhall board.
