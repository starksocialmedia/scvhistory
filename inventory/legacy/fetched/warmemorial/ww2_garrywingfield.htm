Garry Wingfield

Narrative: PVT Garry Wingfield of Soledad Township served with the 31st Infantry Regiment and was killed in action June 13, 1942. PVT Wingfield was awarded the Bronze Star and Purple Heart. He is buried in the Manila American Cemetery, Manila, Philippines.
