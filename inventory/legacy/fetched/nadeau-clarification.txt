 Remi Nadeau: Which is Which? 
 By Marylin Nadeau Chrisman 
 SCVHistory.com | August 11, 2003. 
 My family has been interested in the Valley history all of our lives.
I was very surprised to see that in several places, a number of people
have confused my great-uncle Remi Nadeau and his deer ranch with my
great-great-grandfather Remi Nadeau, the teamster.
 Grandpa Nadeau was the grandfather of the owner
of the deer ranch. My father and his siblings became the owners upon the death
of my Uncle Rem in 1941.
 Both of these men were a part of the early history
of your valley and each played their own unique part. It would be appropriate
to see that their individual contributions were recognized.
 Thanks so much,
 — Marilyn Nadeau Chrisman
