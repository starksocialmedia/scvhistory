 Jill Klajic 
 Santa Clarita City Council 
Click image to enlarge
 Mary Jillene "Jill" Klajic (b. June 14, 1946). Resident of Canyon Country, Newhall and Valencia during council service.
Santa Clarita City Council member, 1990-1994 and 1996-2000. Mayor in 1992.
