# Titles against the scans, batch 1 of 4 (7 October 2026)

For Nathan's read, one at a time. Nothing written. The rule (Nathan, 7 October 2026): newspapers and magazines take what the scan prints, with Leon's page headline kept in its own field; ephemera take the printed cover or masthead title where one exists ("Menu" and "Program Book" name the kind of record, not the title); envelopes, letters and inner pages keep a description.

This batch: the 16 records whose printed headline appears nowhere in the record (the 18 less #4607 and #4751, reverted tonight), then four newspaper and magazine titles in different words.

Conventions in the proposals: an all-capitals headline is set in title case with its words and order unchanged, and a closing period is dropped, as in today's retitle. Where Leon's headline leaves the title: on a photograph it is already in the catalogue entry, every word; on a document or article it goes in a new field, "Headline on SCVHistory.com" (legacyHeadline), created with the first write.
