# College of the Canyons trustees: roster and archive match, 5 October 2026

Read only. The Santa Clarita Community College District's governing board, 1967 to 2026, every trustee matched against the archive's records. Data: `coc-trustees-roster-2026-10-05.json`; new sources saved in `inventory/news/coc-trustees-2026-10-05/roster/` with `manifest.json`.

**35 trustees: 2 SAME, 1 POSSIBLE, 32 NEW.** One more name (Adrian Adams) is claimed by a caption and is not counted; see Disputed.

## Findings that change the record

- **Don Allen was a trustee** (elected spring 1973, resigned before March 1975). The district's board history omits him. Sources: The Canyon Call of May 1, 1973 and February 1975; Boyer (2015).
- **The 1973 contest was two elections.** Spring 1973: Fortine re-elected, Allen elected. November 6, 1973: Carl Boyer elected, 2,646 of about 9,000 ballots, over six others. Neither return was found.
- **Fortine left the board in 1973-1974** and came back by 1992. He was not a member at the April 1974 dedication (Boyer) and was the college's director of community services by late 1974. Leon Worden's AA7401 caption calls Fortine and Adrian Adams trustees in April 1974. Boyer, The Canyon Call and the board history all disagree.
- **There were five cancelled contests, not four:** Trustee Area 1 in 2018 was also cancelled (County list saved). Its appointee (Berger) is inferred.
- **Other contests found:** 1972 full count; March 4, 1975 (four seats, 13 candidates); 1977; 1979; November 1981 with counts (Benton over Boyer, Cubbage over Lynch). See below.
- **Other offices:** Boyer (City Council, #15808), Wilk (Assembly and Senate, #335), MacGregor (Sulphur Springs board before 1993, not in the archive), Berger (Planning Commission 1994-2008, legacy page only), Lynch (Castaic Lake Water Agency), Benton (Washington legislature), Broyles (Canyon County supervisor-elect 1978).

## Notes

- Count: the district's board history names 34 people; the archive's sources add Don Allen (trustee 1973-1975, omitted by the district). Total 35. Nathan's figure of 'forty' is not reached; the board history's gaps (1970, 1979, and the four-name years 1974-1975, 1982-1984, 1984-1985) could hide others, and no source read names any.
- Student trustees: the district's board history lists none, and none is treated here as a voting member. Don Benton was a student trustee before his elected terms (Boyer); Ryan Joslin was student trustee in 2013 (co1310a).
- Terms before 1995 rest on the board history's yearly lists, which say who sat, not how they came or left; where no other source speaks, the term says 'not found'.
- Wikipedia was not used as a source. The only Wikipedia-matching fact is Wilk's appointment date of August 23, 2006, which #335 states without a source: an unsourced lead.

## 1973

The research file's single '1973' contest was two elections. The return of neither was found.

- **Spring 1973** (1973, spring (reported in The Canyon Call of May 1, 1973 as "recent school district elections"; exact date not found)): the two terms expiring in 1973, held by Bruce Fortine and John Hackney (The Signal, June 23, 1972). Bruce Fortine re-elected to a four-year term; Don Allen, a COC student, elected (to the seat Hackney had held, inferred). Votes: not found. Sources: SG720623, CC730501.
- **November 6, 1973:** Carl Boyer elected, "defeated six candidates, receiving 2,646 of slightly more than 9,000 ballots". Seat: not stated; Fortine left the board between May 1973 and April 1974 (not a member at the April 1974 dedication per Boyer; COC director of community services by late 1974), so a contest for his seat is a likely reading, not a read fact. Other candidates: six, not named in the OCR read. Sources: CC7311, BOYER7, BOYER4, CC7412.
- The district's board history lists Fortine and Hackney in 1973 and, for 1974-1975, only Claffey, Muhl, Boyer and Huntsinger: it omits Don Allen entirely.
- Searched: Mirror /Volumes/Reggie/SCVHistory/scvhistory.com, Python cp1252 over every .htm/.html/.txt, 5 October 2026, for each trustee name and 'COC|College of the Canyons|college' + 'board|trustee'; the Canyon Call OCR volumes 1971-1979 read for 'trustee', 'election', 'resign', candidates' names; Boyer's 2015 book (all chapter PDFs via pdftotext). No Signal report of either 1973 return is on the mirror.
