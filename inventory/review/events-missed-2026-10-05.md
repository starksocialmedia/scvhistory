## How this was done

Leon Worden's own structure was the map, in this order:

1. **His chronology**, `scvhistory/timeline.htm` ("SCV Chronology: A Timeline of Historical Events, by Leon Worden"). It has about 1,400 dated lines from the Holocene to 2024, and it is the best single source for this question. Every date below without another source comes from it.
2. **The Signal's 80-year timeline**, `scvhistory/sg19191999.htm` ("Timeline: SCV History in The Signal, 1919-1999"), and `scvhistory/review1981.htm` ("Year in Review: 1981").
3. **The General Interest index**, `scvhistory/general.htm`, which has its own headings: NATURE-EARTHQUAKES-FLOODS, AIRCRAFT DOWN, FIRE FIGHTING, LAW ENFORCEMENT & MILITARY. Under each heading Leon lists the events he treats as events, with their dates.
4. **Topic labels.** Every page title has the form `SCVHistory.com | Topic | Description`, and many pages carry a breadcrumb (`> FIRES/DISASTERS > LAKE HUGHES`). I counted these across all 9,429 content pages (the 24,000 flipbook files under `scvhistory/files/` were left out). Topics that name an event include Powerhouse Fire (106), 1929 Saugus Train Robbery (16 plus 29 more pages by title), 1971 Earthquake (14), Aircraft Down (26), Plane Crashes (19), Castaic Range War (6 plus a 24-page newspaper series), El Nino 1997-98 (5), 2003 Simi Fire (5), Station Fire (6), Great Flood of 1938 (3), 1962 Melody Ranch Fire (3) and Saugus Rodeo (5).
5. **Keyword sweeps** of page titles and page text for each prompt in the brief.

**Page counts.** "Titled" means pages whose `<title>` names the event. "Mentions" means pages whose text names it. Mention counts run high, because Leon's SEE ALSO boxes repeat on every page in a series. For Craft I matched record titles. Where Craft has only a passing mention (a Reynolds chapter, for example), I give the ID. Photo descriptions that mention an event only inside a copied navigation box are not counted.

**Ranks.** A: major, recurs across the sources, would carry a full record. B: a short record. C: a line on another record, or a mention.

**Not found.** The phrase "There it is. Take it." does not appear on any mirror page. The aqueduct opening is covered (see below), but that quote would need another source. The Newhall Pass interchange collapses (1971 and 1994) appear only as parts of those two earthquakes, not as separate events. Nothing local turned up for the Twenty Mule Team (the borax pages are about Tick Canyon's Sterling Borax mine) or for a Santa Clarita Metrolink crash.

## 1. Disasters

### Fires

**Powerhouse Fire** (A)
- Date: broke out 30 May 2013 near Power House No. 1 in San Francisquito Canyon; burned to 11 June 2013 (governor's proclamation).
- Mirror: 106 pages under the topic "Powerhouse Fire". Examples: `scvhistory/lw2382a.htm` "Powerhouse Fire Perimeter Map"; `scvhistory/lw2386b.htm` "Lost Homes in Lake Hughes"; `scvhistory/brown_powerhouseproclamation.htm` "Gov. Brown Declares State of Emergency in Los Angeles County, 7-12-2013".

**Castaic Range War (Jenkins-Chormicle feud)** (A)

## 5. Recurring events and others

## Open points for Nathan

1. **One source fault to log.** The AIRCRAFT DOWN index on `general.htm` dates the Twilight Zone crash "1-27-1982". The NTSB report and Pollack give July 1982 (23 July, and 22 July for the night filming began).
2. **Flight 7 casualty conflict.** The chronology says "2 dead, 11 injured"; the 1937 headline page says "5 Killed". The record needs both, attributed.
3. **Fixed events versus campaigns.** Elsmere, Cemex, Canyon County and the range war are campaigns that ran for years, not single days. They need either a start and end date on one record, or a decision that they belong as organization or place records instead.
