# The 18 assets "whose original download was never kept" (overnight, 8 October 2026)

Claude, read only, for Nathan (item 9: "The 18 assets whose original download was never kept. Try Archive.org and the usual sources for each."). Nothing was set in Craft; nothing was committed.

## The answer

**All 18 originals are held in the repository already, byte for byte.** Every recorded `sourceChecksum` matches a file on disk, and every one of those files is the same picture as the stored asset at the same pixel size. None of them was lost. The class came from the checksum audit (scripts/import/checksum_audit_2026_10_08.py), which looked in too few places and hashed the wrong way for four of them:

1. **It looked in four folders only** (inventory/incoming, inventory/sole-copies, inventory/elections, storage/masters). Fourteen originals sit elsewhere: inventory/marks (7), inventory/sources/water-marks-2026-10-04 (3), inventory/news/city-commissions-2026-10-04/portraits (2) and storage/runtime/photo-import/wayback (2).
2. **It hashed every held file with SHA-256 only**, then compared that with the recorded value after removing the `sha1:` prefix. Four records carry a SHA-1 (the Commons and Flickr files, because Commons publishes SHA-1): #26976 McKeon, #21863 CSUN, #21861 City Hall, #21761 Scofield. A SHA-256 can never equal a SHA-1, so those four could never match, although three of them sat in inventory/sole-copies and one in inventory/incoming, both folders it did walk.

Every held file predates the audit (the latest is 7 October, 19:39).

## How it was checked

What was read, before any number:

- **The file itself:** every image and PDF under inventory/, storage/ and web/uploads/ (17,897 files), plus ~/Downloads and ~/Desktop, hashed with both SHA-256 and SHA-1 and matched against the 18 recorded checksums.
- **The file itself:** each stored asset and its held original, decoded with Imagick, flattened on white and compared pixel by pixel at the stored size (RMSE and peak error, 0 to 1 scale).
- **A record, not the file:** the Craft fields on each asset (source, sourceUrl, legacySourcePath, sourceChecksum, provenanceKind), and the ids of the class, from storage/runtime/photo-import/checksum-audit.json.
- **The source, independently of us:** the Commons API's own SHA-1 for the three Commons files; the Flickr original, downloaded again; the Wayback CDX payload digests (base32 SHA-1, computed by the Internet Archive) for lw2724a, lw2724b, the SCVHS logo and the 2008 water quality report; and the live files on the bodies' own sites for six school and water marks and the two City portraits. Downloads are in the scratchpad (overnight/originals18), not in the repo. scvhistory.com itself was not fetched; only its Wayback captures were read.

By eye: lw2724a (stored and capture) and the Saugus mark (stored and original) were viewed side by side: the same picture.

Pixel results: the ten marks are identical (RMSE 0.0000) except Saugus (RMSE 0.0049, peak 0.22, at the anti-aliased edge where transparency is flattened); the photographs differ only by Craft's re-encoding (RMSE 0.0007 to 0.0036); the two lw2724 captures by 0.017, because Craft re-saved them much smaller (224 KB and 80 KB against 936 KB and 311 KB). Same picture, same size, every one.
