## The contradictions and tensions, first

Each is set out again under its rule below. Nothing here has been acted on.

1. **The Firefly-edit rule and the 17 put back.** The rule of 8 October, late: "Any portrait where Firefly filled, removed, cleaned, or made an edit the credential does not describe comes off the record", with the reason "A credential that will not say what changed cannot support a claim that the face is the person's" (pull_firefly_edits_2026_10_08.php). The same evening the 17 went back on your word ("Restore the 17 to the enhanced pair"). Of those 17, 11 are undescribed Firefly Image 5 edits (Carey, Darcy, Boyer, Rioux, Tom Mix, Manly, Lynch, Ruth Newhall, Pederson, Mulholland, Lyon), and 6 carry a fill or a removal (Leon Worden, Richman, Couts, Scofield, Perkins's removed shoulder; Frémont's corners, seen painted in on the sheet). So all 17 fail the rule as it is written, and the rule has not been rewritten. The only line between the 17 and the portraits that stayed off (Steve Knight, the Runners, Messina, Jensen) is that the 17 are your own work. That line is written nowhere as a rule.
2. **The Firefly-edit rule is in no rule document.** HANDOFF says "The rule and its reasons are in docs/DATA-MODEL.md ('Generated and edited images')". DATA-MODEL holds the generated-image rule of 8 October, but not the Firefly-edit rule. It still says the opposite: "A crop, an upscale, a removed bystander, a cleaned background or a cropped edge filled in is ordinary archival practice" (1 October). The Firefly-edit rule lives only in CHANGELOG, the pull script's header, inventory/review/generated-images-2026-10-08.md and the HANDOFF caution.
3. **The enhanced-pair rule against the Firefly-edit rule, on the original not held.** DATA-MODEL (6 October): "Where the original is not held, `enhancedFrom` stays empty and the caption says so." The rule of 8 October: edits with no original held come off ("A record with no portrait is honest"). Both are in force on paper.
4. **The enlargements kept.** López, Wicks and Kellar keep Firefly generative enlargements as portraits. DATA-MODEL says an upscale "has pixels a model invented" ("Images as objects"). Each enlargement's own recorded method says "Generative steps can add detail the photograph did not record".
