# City Managers of the City of Santa Clarita, 1987 to 2026

Research for Nathan, 4 October 2026 (Claude, read-only). Nothing was written to the database.

Question: who has been City Manager of the City of Santa Clarita since incorporation (15 December 1987), with dates of appointment and departure, how each left, and any interim or acting managers.

Saved sources: `inventory/news/city-managers-2026-10-04/` (25 files, with `manifest.json` giving URL, read date, sha256, bytes and what each holds). File names below refer to that folder.

## Answer

Four people have held the post. Two of those terms were interim: Fred Bien in 1987 and 1988, and Ken Pulskamp in 2002 before his permanent appointment. Leon Worden called Striplin "only the third permanent city manager" in 2012 (SCVNews, 30 Aug 2012), which fits this list.

No acting or interim manager was found in 2012. Pulskamp's last day was 30 November and Striplin's contract was amended to start on 1 December so the City would not "go without a city manager for a month." I also found no acting manager between Bien and Caravalho in 1988, though the gap has not been checked to the day.

## Uncertain or conflicting

1. **Bien's end date:** NEEDS_VERIFICATION. It follows from Caravalho's start ("May 1988" on the City biography). Neither the day nor how Bien's engagement ended (contract interim or otherwise) is documented in the sources read. Nothing is known of Bien beyond this role, and no person record is proposed. Whether he meets the bar for a full record is Nathan's call.
2. **Bien's start date:** "the day after the cityhood vote" (4 November 1987) comes from Leon Worden's note. He was hired by the council-elect before the city legally existed, so the City Manager title formally begins on 15 December 1987. McKeon's Winter 1987-88 piece confirms he was hired and at work before incorporation.
3. **Caravalho's dates to the day:** NEEDS_VERIFICATION. The only month-level source is the City biography reproduced on SC9020. The Riverside City Clerk's records (aquarius.riversideca.gov) should give his Riverside appointment date. Those were not read.
4. **Caravalho after Santa Clarita:** the CCMF statement of January 2020 says he "transitioned to a role as the director of Dana Point Harbor in 2002." The obituary and SC9020 say Riverside from 2002 to 2005, then Dana Point Harbor from 2005 to 2007. The obituary and SC9020 agree, and CCMF looks wrong.
5. **Pulskamp's permanent appointment date:** NEEDS_VERIFICATION. It falls between 9 July 2002 (still interim) and the 6 February 2003 capture (City Manager). A search engine returned `scvhistory.com/scvhistory/cc091202minutes.pdf` (council minutes, apparently from September 2002), with a snippet reading "Ken Pulskamp, Interim City Manager." The file is not in the Reggie mirror or in the Wayback Machine, and I did not fetch it from the live site. If those minutes are from 10 or 12 September 2002, he was still interim then. The City's own council minutes for September to December 2002 would settle it.
6. **Striplin's council actions:** the selection on 30 August 2012 is reported. The contract vote (scheduled for 11 September 2012) and the start-date amendment (scheduled for 13 November 2012) are reported only as agenda items. Their adoption NEEDS_VERIFICATION in the minutes.
7. **Ages and birth dates:** the legacy pages print full birth dates for Pulskamp and Striplin. Both are living, so only the year would be usable. None is used here.

## Living people

Pulskamp and Striplin are living. This note covers only their public posts. Since 2012 Pulskamp has held other public posts: interim City Manager of Burbank from December 2012, and later Executive Director of the California City Management Foundation (per search results). Neither post is verified here, and neither is a Santa Clarita post.
