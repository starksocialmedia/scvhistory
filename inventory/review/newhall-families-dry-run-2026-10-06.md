What this means in plain words:

- **Scott Newhall is Henry Mayo Newhall's great-grandson**, through Edwin White Newhall (Henry's third son) and Almer Mayo Newhall. His own oral history says so (p. 1: "The survivor was my grandfather, Edwin Newhall"; p. 4: "My great-grandfather on my father's side, Henry Mayo Newhall"), and so do a 1936 wire caption, The Signal in 1963, LW3325, LW2915 and LW3564.
- **Ruth Newhall** is a Newhall by marriage to Scott, from 1933 to his death.
- **The two Walters**: Walter Scott Newhall (1860 to 1906) was Henry's fourth son and Scott's great-uncle (Scott, p. 322: "a great-aunt who had married my great-uncle Walter"). Walter Scott Newhall (1908 to 1960) was Henry's grandson through George Almer Newhall, the "youngest grandson" (RN0114), and a first cousin of Scott's father.
- **Jane Newhall** (1913 to 2011), the hospital and Boys & Girls Club benefactor, was Scott's half first cousin. Scott: "although we have different grandmothers, we have the same grandfather" (oral history p. 334).
- **Atholl McBean and Peter McBean** are family by marriage and descent: Atholl married Henry's granddaughter Margaret (LW3325; "grandson-in-law," COC Silver Spur 2015); Peter, their son, called himself Henry's great-grandson at the 1950 pool dedication. California Business (1989) calls Scott McBean's "nephew"; the tree makes him the son of Atholl's wife's first cousin. Do not use "nephew."
- **The Chesebroughs** descend from Henry through William Mayo Newhall's daughter Elizabeth.
- **J.O. Newhall** was a relative, but the sources disagree on how: "either a brother (per Ruth Newhall) or cousin (per Andrew Rolle)" (AP1006); "an Eastern cousin" (Rolle p. 99, quoted on AP0109); "cousin to Henry Mayo Newhall" (A.B. Perkins, The Signal, January 2, 1947, quoted on AP2704); "brother of town founder" (AP0403). Unsettled; no record.

## 3. Who only shares the name

**No person in the archive shares the Newhall name without being family.** Every person named Newhall in the mirror's text is in the tree above, married into it, or J.O. (related, degree unsettled). I checked every "<Name> Newhall" form in the text index: the others are places and things named for Henry Mayo Newhall, not people: the town of Newhall, The Newhall Signal, the Newhall Land and Farming Company, Henry Mayo Newhall Memorial Hospital, Newhall Ranch, Newhall Pass, the Newhall schools, the Jan Heidt Newhall Metrolink Station (named for the town and for Jan Heidt, who is not a Newhall). So the valley's assumption is right for the people the archive holds, but it holds the line through Edwin, which the town's other landmarks do not show.

The real risk of confusion is within the family: two Walter Scott Newhalls, two George Almer Newhalls, two Almer Mayo Newhalls, and two William Mayo Newhalls. The draft gives Scott a "Not to be confused with" note for the Walters.
