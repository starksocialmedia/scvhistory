## 2. A Communities tab on the City's page

### (a) Community records

There is no communities section. Communities are the category group `neighborhood` ("Communities"), 35 terms, all enabled, flat (no parent levels). Fields on the group: body, Also Known As (`communityAliases`), `communityType`, `communityLat`, `communityLng`, images, documents, named for, naming note, cultural sensitivity note.

- `communityType` is **empty on all 35**.
- No boundary field exists on the terms. Boundaries live in a file, `web/data/communities.geojson`, keyed by slug (19 features), with method and caveats recorded in its metadata.
- 26 terms have coordinates; 9 do not (Fair Oaks Ranch, Mint Canyon, Saugus/Valencia, Haskell Canyon, Mojave Desert, Potrero Canyon, Towsley Canyon, Ravenna, Soledad Township; TODO.md already lists six of them). The coordinates were set by `scripts/import/set_community_coords.php` as the **centroid of each community's polygon** in communities.geojson, or Wikipedia where there is no polygon.
- **Membership already exists as data.** The City record (#394, "The City of Santa Clarita") relates, through its `neighborhood` field: Newhall #199, Saugus #205, Valencia #211, Canyon Country #190, Fair Oaks Ranch #193, Sand Canyon #203. That is the "in" list, and it is a curated fact, not a geometry test. The City's page already draws the City outline (`ORG_BOUNDARIES` in `templates/organizations/_entry.twig` maps `city-of-santa-clarita` to the `santa-clarita` feature).

Polygons in communities.geojson and how much of each lies inside the City (sampled on a 0.002 degree grid against the union of the City's five council districts):

The points, tested against the City:

- In: Newhall, Saugus, Valencia, Santa Clarita, Soledad Canyon.
- **Out, though the communities are in the City: Canyon Country (34.402, -118.383) and Sand Canyon (34.400, -118.403).** Canyon Country's point is the centroid of two ZIP areas that run far east into unincorporated land; Sand Canyon's is the centroid of the small unincorporated remnant. Wikipedia's points for Canyon Country (34.42, -118.45), Newhall (34.38, -118.53), Saugus (34.44, -118.52) and Valencia (34.42, -118.56) all fall inside the City. Sand Canyon and Fair Oaks Ranch have no Wikipedia coordinates.
- Out, correctly: Castaic, Val Verde, Agua Dulce, Stevenson Ranch, and the rest.

### (b) A City boundary

- `web/data/communities.geojson`, feature `santa-clarita`: from the County CSA layer "City of Santa Clarita", 182.9 km2 (70.6 sq mi). **It is out of date.** The live CSA feature is the same size (183.5 km2), so the County has not updated it for the **Tesoro del Valle annexation (14 November 2022, 1,785 acres)**. The 7.6 km2 the council districts hold beyond it sits at about 34.48, -118.56, which is Tesoro del Valle.
- `inventory/sources/trustee-areas-2026-10-04/city-of-santa-clarita/city-gis-city-council-districts-layer17.geojson` (City GIS) and `rrcc-division-boundaries-santa-clarita-council.geojson` (County RR/CC): five council districts, 190.2 km2 together. Dissolved, they are the current City outline. The same districts are already published as `web/data/seats/city-of-santa-clarita.geojson`. Licence note in the manifest: the City GIS copy is "non-commercial, may not be mirrored without written permission"; the RR/CC copy is under the County's open terms. **Use the RR/CC copy.**
- The layer the manifest calls the boundary mapserver (Boundary/MapServer/17) is the council districts, not the city limit. The City's server also has:
  - `Boundary/MapServer/0` "City of Santa Clarita Boundary": one polyline feature, 47,137 acres (73.65 sq mi, 190.8 km2). It agrees with the council districts.
  - `Boundary/MapServer/14` "Annexation": 45 dated polygons, from Morton Stanley (18 December 1989) to Tesoro del Valle (14 November 2022), plus two marked "Potential" (Eastside, Gateway Ranch). Taking the annexations away from today's outline gives roughly the 1987 city, which would let the tab show how the City grew. This needs Nathan's word, and the City's mirroring terms apply.
  - Not saved: this research was limited to writing under inventory/review/.

### (c) Official community boundaries

- **The County CSAs do not divide the City.** The City is one CSA, "City of Santa Clarita". The CSAs named Newhall, Saugus, Valencia, Canyon Country, Sand Canyon and "Saugus/Canyon Country" are all "Unincorporated - ...", meaning the parts of those communities outside the City. They are useful for the "out" side and for showing that part of a community lies outside the City. They cannot be used to draw the in-City communities.
- **The City publishes no community layer.** I listed every service on maps.santa-clarita.com (Boundary, Zones, Administrative, Property, Single_Use_Map_Service, SCV_History, Hosted, OldData). None has a community, neighbourhood or village layer. The nearest things are specific-plan and corridor polygons (Old Town Newhall, North Valencia I and II, Soledad Corridor, Lyons Corridor, Town Center) and Homeowners Associations, and none of those is a community. This agrees with the communities.geojson caveat: "The City of Santa Clarita publishes no community or planning-area layer."
- **What the archive uses now is ZIP approximations** (ZCTAs). As the table shows, they do not follow the City line: two thirds of the Canyon Country shape is outside the City.

So no official polygon exists for Newhall, Saugus, Valencia, Canyon Country or Sand Canyon as parts of the City.

### Simplest honest way to draw it

**The City outline as the one polygon, the in-City communities as labelled points inside it, and the out communities as their County CSA outlines in a muted style.**

1. **City:** dissolve the RR/CC council districts into one outline (current, includes Tesoro del Valle), and replace the stale CSA `santa-clarita` feature with it. That one change also fixes the outline the City's page draws today.
2. **In-City communities:** the six on the City record's `neighborhood` relation, as points with labels only. No polygons, because no source draws them. A note under the map: "The City does not publish community boundaries. Parts of Newhall, Saugus, Valencia, Canyon Country and Sand Canyon lie outside the City in unincorporated county territory."
3. **Out communities:** Castaic, Val Verde, Agua Dulce, Stevenson Ranch (and the other unincorporated neighbours) drawn from their existing CSA polygons, which really are County-defined unincorporated areas.
4. **In or out comes from the relation on #394, never from a point-in-polygon test**, because two of the current points would test wrongly.

What has to be fixed first:

- **Label positions for Canyon Country and Sand Canyon.** Their current coordinates fall outside the City. The polygon-centroid rule in `set_community_coords.php` is wrong for ZCTA communities that reach far into backcountry. Wikipedia's Canyon Country point (34.42, -118.45) is inside the City. Sand Canyon and Fair Oaks Ranch need a sourced point (USGS GNIS populated-place coordinates are public domain); do not place them by hand. Newhall's centroid (34.365, -118.492) is inside the City but sits at the south-east edge of the ZIP area; Wikipedia's (34.38, -118.53) is nearer the old town.
- **Fair Oaks Ranch** has no coordinates at all (TODO.md).
- **The City feature** is pre-2022, as above.
- `communityType` is empty everywhere. It is not needed for this tab because the relation carries the in or out.

Not recommended: clipping the ZCTAs to the City line to make in-City polygons. They would look official, but they are ZIP-code shapes with a visible seam (Canyon Country and Valencia are each two ZCTAs). Drawing them inside the City would present an approximation as a boundary.
