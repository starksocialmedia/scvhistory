Agent: Claude. Read-only: no database writes, no edits to existing files.

## Question

Nathan, 5 October 2026: 27 office-holding footnotes quoted Education Code 5328 with an ellipsis that joined the nominee clause to the appointee clause ("as if elected"). "Check whether any other statute or document quotation is spliced the same way."

## Scope and method

- Craft: every live entry, category and asset; every Table-field cell (footnotes, editorNotes, recordDates) and every text field. 703 field values contain an ellipsis. Of those, the archive's own writing is the 126 footnote rows with source `editorial-2026` (no editorNotes, recordDates notes, webmasterNote, sourceFault basis or editorial-2026 body carries a quoted ellipsis). Legacy bodies (photographs, articles, documents, obituaries, war memorial legacyHtml) are the source's own words and out of scope.
- Drafts not yet applied: the seven files `inventory/review/*-draft-*.json` and `*profiles*.json`.
- Each quotation was split at its ellipses, each piece found in the source, and the omitted text read. Sources: the legacy mirror (named pages only), Craft records, `inventory/` copies (County lists, Board of Governors item, Ordinance 23-4), and the cited web pages fetched on 5 October 2026.
- Classes: **SPLICE** (meaning changed), **RISKY** (joins sentences, paragraphs or clauses, or omits without a mark, but meaning holds; better as separate quotations), **FINE** (omission inside one sentence, list or table row), **UNCHECKED** (source not reachable).
- In source passages, whitespace is normalised and "[...]" marks this audit's own cut for length, not the source's.

## Findings in brief

- **One SPLICE, and it is the same Education Code 5328 splice.** Office holding #28497 (Denis DeFigueiredo, Sulphur Springs, 2007) footnote row 0 still reads: the nominee "shall be seated at the organizational meeting of the board ... as if elected at a district election." The 5 October fix (`unopposed_not_appointed_2026_10_05.php`) listed 27 IDs; #28497 was not among them because the appointed-terms audit judged its 2007 claim UNSUPPORTED, so its quotation was never corrected. No other statute quotation in the archive uses an ellipsis. The other "as if elected" text in live records (#27410 Gibbs) quotes Elections Code 10229(a) in two separate quotations with no ellipsis, both from subdivision (a), and is accurate.
- **No other legal-document quotation changes meaning.** The court judgment (Agua Dulce Town Council, #29860), the 2013 and 2016 County candidate lists, the Board of Governors item and Ordinance 23-4 are elided inside a clause or list. The 1869 drilling agreement (#20228 row 1) joins clauses and drops the profit-sharing term, but each kept clause stays with its own subject: RISKY, not SPLICE.
- **RISKY, 18:** mostly a "..." standing for one or more whole sentences. The nearest to a splice is #28940 row 1 and #25409 row 7 (Measure V oversight committee): the ellipsis joins the first sentence to the tail of the third, so "approved by local voters in 2001 for $158 million" now modifies "school bond funds" instead of "the measure". The fact is the same, so it stays RISKY. #26999 row 4 (bioguide) also drops a whole item with no ellipsis.
- **Attribution, not splicing:** #394 row 4 quotes "In 2024 ... District 1 and District 3." after the City's District Elections page citation, but the words are Ordinance 23-4 section 2.B (cited earlier in the same footnote); the page does not carry them.
- **UNCHECKED, 3:** the three California Digital Newspaper Collection quotations (#21584 rows 7 and 11, #16039 row 13). cdnc.ucr.edu answered 403 (Cloudflare) to curl and WebFetch, with no Wayback capture and no local copy. All three are short phrase-level elisions.
- Leginfo also answered with a Cloudflare challenge; the 5328 text below is from california.public.law, which reproduces leginfo and matches the opening words quoted from leginfo in the fix script.
