# William S. Hart (1864?–1946): source dossier for SCVHistory.com / Craft migration

Read-only research, compiled 2026-09-29 (CEST). All scvhistory.com pages cited were fetched live on 2026-09-29 between 05:48 and 06:10 CEST (328 URLs, 327 HTTP 200). Anything not checked against a source is marked **NEEDS_VERIFICATION**. Outside references are finding aids, not authorities.

## Summary

* **Dates.** Birth: **Dec 6, 1864, Newburgh NY** has the best support: the 1880 census gives age 16 (lw3336), and Wikipedia, Wikidata Q636680 and Find a Grave agree. But the site's own pages give 1862 (LA Examiner obituary), "around 1870" (Reynolds), and 1868/1871/1873/1874 (Hart's own claims), and NHMLA text hedges "probably 1864". Death: **June 23, 1946**, 11:20 pm, at California Lutheran Hospital, **Los Angeles** (LAE 6-24-1946). Wikidata, Find a Grave and Wikipedia say Newhall; the LAT 2004 Hart Jr. obituary says June 24.
* **Newhall timeline (best evidence).** Leased the Babcock Smith ranch in 1918. First purchase Feb 1921. The ranch was assembled in about 10 transactions through Oct 1933 (Sitton 1989, citing deed books). So the site's "254 acres bought in 1921" is anachronistic. Mansion designed from July 1926 and finished late 1927 / early 1928, not 1925 (NHMLA) or 1930 (cp1703). American Theater: announced and deeded 1940, opened May 23, 1941. Funeral at the ranch house and Forest Lawn, then cremation, then Green-Wood (Brooklyn).
* **Conflicts:** 22 numbered groups (C1–C22) in Section 3, plus 4 open questions. The biggest: birth year; death place and date; cremation vs "body by train"; ranch purchase and acreage (eight different acreages); mansion dates; the St. Francis Dam "little victim" legend (Reynolds) vs contemporary Signal reporting (sg051703); the "Rags" claim (Hart is not in the AFI cast); and six verified index/caption errors.
* **Custody has changed.** The County approved transfer of Hart Park to the City of Santa Clarita (Aug 6, 2024). Master Agreement Feb 13, 2025; probate court accepted May 2025; City ownership July 14, 2025. NHM's P-75 and P-98 photo collections were transferred to the City. The Hart Papers (GC 1012) and Park research files (GC 1192) are still listed at NHM's Seaver Center on OAC. Site text naming LA County/NHMLA as operator is outdated.
* **Retrofit (2026-09-29):** Section 6 lists 13 live errors on the site, Section 7 lists 5 cases where the archive beats outside references, and Section 8 holds corrections (the Westover link was not confirmed).
* **Counts:** 50 local + 5 written at the ranch + 30 borderline = 85 pages in full detail. Leon's master Hart index (film.htm) has 292 links, of which 286 are content entries (284 distinct targets). 222 index entries are not in the local set and are listed compactly. Details in Section 1.

## 3. Conflicts

### C1. Birth year (1864 vs 1862/1868/1870/1871/1872/1873/1874)

* **lw3336**: 1880 census (312 E 73rd St, NYC): William age 16 (page: "born in December of 1864"). Father Nicholas 46, miller, b. England; mother Rosanna 41, b. Ireland
* **barnes_hartfamily2014**: 1870 and 1880 censuses: Hart was the second child, after Nettie (b. c.1860-61); "popped on the scene in 1864"
* **cp1702 / mu9067 / cp1703 / lw3041 / lw3169**: NHMLA boilerplate: born "probably in 1864"
* **reynolds part53**: "around December 6, 1870"; a footnote says the year is disputed and that Friends of Hart Park use 1864
* **lat19360719hart**: Hart, July 1936: "I'll be 64 in December" (implies 1872). Ed. note: "Hart always lied about his age"
* **lw3335**: Hart gave his birth year variously as "most often 1868, 1871 or 1874"; the son's 1922 birth certificate implies 1874
* **lw2318a**: Hart claimed December 1873; AP 1944 called him "70-year-old"; Newburgh birth records "seem to have been lost to fire" (NEEDS_VERIFICATION)
* **lae62446a / lae62446c**: "December 6, 1862", "He was 83" (LA Examiner obituary). Ed. note: "By most accounts... 1864"
* **lahe06261946c**: "82-year-old" (fits 1863/64)
* **lw2294a**: ACME, Apr 1940: "William S. Hart, 75" (fits 1864)
* **lw2291**: "81½-year life"; married Dec 7, 1921, "the day after his 57th birthday" (fits 1864)
* **Outside:** Wikipedia, Wikidata Q636680 (P569 1864-12-06, sourced to GND, BnF, Find a Grave, IBDB, SNAC, Proleksis) and Find a Grave 456 all give Dec 6, 1864 at Newburgh, NY. The NHM Seaver GC 1012 catalog hedges: "(1864?-1946)".
* **Evidence favors:** 1864. The 1880 census age (16) and the family order in the 1870 census (per Barnes) fit it, as do the 1940, 1946 (Herald-Express) and 1950 press ages. The later years came from Hart himself shaving his age. Day and month (Dec 6) are not disputed on the site.

### C2. Sister Mary Ellen: birth year

* **lw3336**: 1880 census: Mary Ellen age 8, b. Illinois (implies c.1872)
* **barnes_hartfamily2014**: "about two years to 1872, and another sister is born... Mary Ellen"; born Aurora per death records
* **lw2295a**: "whose exact birth date is uncertain"; died Oct 1, 1943; buried Green-Wood Oct 14, 1943
* **Outside:** Find a Grave gives her as 1878-1943.
* **Evidence favors:** c.1872 (census, cited on two site pages); the 1878 on Find a Grave is probably wrong (NEEDS_VERIFICATION).

### C3. Middle name: Surrey / Surry / Shakespeare

* **lw3335**: the son's birth certificate reads "William Surry Hart Jr."; "we've never been 100-percent certain about the old man's name"
* **obituary_williamshartjr**: LAT 2004: father was "William Surrey Hart"
* **(Photoplay April 1921, cited in site research)**: middle name given as "Shakespeare"
* **Outside:** Wikipedia and Wikidata: "William Surrey Hart". The NHM GC 1012 title is "William Surrey Hart". The LC name authority n80046059 is heading "Hart, William S. (William Surrey)" per the NHM ArchivesSpace subject (NEEDS_VERIFICATION against id.loc.gov).
* **Evidence favors:** "Surrey" by consensus, but no primary birth document has been seen. Treat "Surry" (1922) as the spelling on the son's legal document only.

### C4. Death date and place

* **lae62446a-d**: died at California Lutheran Hospital, Los Angeles, at 11:20 p.m., June 23, 1946; hospitalized since June 5
* **lw2291**: agrees on the 1946 death
* **obituary_williamshartjr**: LAT 5-25-2004: "Hart Sr. died June 24, 1946"
* **Outside:** Wikidata P570 1946-06-23 and P20 Newhall; Find a Grave says died in Newhall; Wikipedia says Newhall.
* **Evidence favors:** June 23, 1946 (the contemporary LAE; the LAT 2004 obituary is wrong or counts from the 6-24 news date). Place: the Los Angeles hospital, not Newhall (Wikidata, Find a Grave and Wikipedia are imprecise).

### C5. Funeral and disposition of remains: cremation vs body by train

* **lahe06261946b / c**: service in the den of the Newhall ranch home (Dr. George Davidson, St. John's Episcopal), then the Little Church of the Recessional at Forest Lawn (Rudy Vallee sang); "Tomorrow evening Hart will be cremated"; ashes held in a Forest Lawn vault about two weeks, then to Green-Wood beside Mary Ellen
* **reynolds part53**: son later sought to exhume the father's brain (hard to square with cremation; inference)
* **Outside:** Find a Grave 456: "his body was transported by train" to Green-Wood, Section 191, Lot 29116.
* **Evidence favors:** Cremation (a contemporary account, 6-26-1946). The Find a Grave wording is unsourced.

### C6. Ranch acquisition: lease 1918, purchase Feb 1921, "254 acres in 1921"

* **fh2701**: "purchased the 254-acre Horseshoe Ranch in Newhall in 1921 from Babcock Smith"
* **reynolds part53**: "February 1921... 254-acre"
* **mu8901**: Sitton 1989: leased from George Babcock Smith in 1918. Feb 1921 bought lots 19-20 of Tract 1059 plus the east halves of lots 17-18 (Newhall Signal 4 Feb 1921; LA Co. Deeds 16:319, as transcribed). Then parcels added July 1921 (Anderson 370:253; Naylor 477:226), 1926 (Quisenberry, Thibaudeau), 4 Apr 1927 (Cooper E Lot 15, 6616:260), Nov 1927 (7982:315), 23 Jun 1932 (Cooper W Lot 15, 11682:113), late 1933 (Purrucker), and 21 Oct 1933 (Bates, Lots 27-29 Tract 2703, 12365:350): "the Horseshoe Ranch was complete"
* **jf3201**: Hart letter to his real-estate agent, 7-22-1932, contemporary with the June 1932 Cooper purchase
* **Outside:** HMDB marker 250177 ("The Ranch House"): built 1910 by Smith, leased 1918, bought 1921. hometownstation.com "Today in SCV History": Feb 5, 1921.
* **Evidence favors:** Lease 1918, first purchase Feb 1921, ranch assembled piecemeal to Oct 1933. "254 acres in 1921" projects the final size back onto the first deed. Feb 4 (Signal issue) vs Feb 5 (hometownstation) is unresolved; the deed recording date would settle it.

### C7. Ranch and park acreage

* **lat19360719hart**: "about 200 acres" (1936)
* **lw3486**: AP 1936: "200 rugged acres"
* **lae62446c**: "80-acre ranch" (1946)

* **fh2701 / reynolds part53**: 254 acres

* **Evidence favors:** Unresolved. The figures describe different things (the 1920s ranch, the 1946 estate, the park after later additions or subtractions). CC should cite a figure with its date and source, never as one fact.
