# Titles against the scans (7 October 2026)

Read-only. Claude. Nothing in Craft was changed. Per-record data in `titles-vs-scans-2026-10-07.json` beside this file; scripts, OCR and previews in `storage/runtime/titles-vs-scans/`.

Question (Nathan): for every record where a scan exists and is legible, does the title we now hold match what the scan prints?

## Coverage

- **Photographs.** All 141 photographs the type census labelled as text pages (article scans, programs, brochures, menus, letters, ads, official papers). Each scan was read by eye, from the flipbook PDF or the largest image on Reggie or in Craft; PDF text and tesseract were used to find the headline page. The 1,404 pictures and objects, and the 17 born-digital web articles on photo pages, print no title and are out of scope.
- **Articles.** All 772 screened. Every image on the legacy page and in Craft was OCR-tested, and large images with no OCR text were checked by eye. 18 carry a scan of printed matter. The 222 COINage pages are missing from the Reggie mirror; only their Craft assets could be checked.
- **Documents.** All 62 screened; 25 compared. The Saugus High School 2019 set carries photographs, not page scans. 14 born-digital or official election PDFs were not compared.
- 175 records in all. 49 scans print no headline (envelopes, letters, invoices, inner program pages, maps). 2 have no scan anywhere (5423, 4435). None was illegible.

"Matches" allows differences of case and punctuation only. Counting shortened or extended forms as well, 87 of 124 (70%) carry the printed words.

**Is the printed headline in the record anyway?** Of the 37 titles in different words, 19 records already hold the printed headline somewhere (body, catalogueCaption or originallyPublishedTitle). This leaves out three trivial hits ("RAMONA", "CHAPTER 2", a book title). 18 do not hold it. Of the 42 shortened or extended titles, 22 hold the full printed form.

## What the evidence shows

1. **For newspapers and magazines, Leon's page headline is his own about a third of the time.** 15 of 47 are in different words. The magazine pieces (4783, 4909, 5087, 4269) were titled by Leon. The clippings imported straight from the scan (the sg110185 page, 8 of 8) match.
2. **On ephemera the title is a description, not the printed words.** The title adds the kind of thing ("Program Book:", "Menu", "Advertisement", "Screenplay:") or drops the issuer. Only 26% match exactly, but most are recognisable extensions.
3. **The retitle mostly helped.** Where the title changed, the old title was closer to the scan only twice: 4607 dropped "Funscoming", and 4751 dropped "Bonelli Stadium". Several old titles were Leon's descriptions, and the new title matches the cover (4893, 4875, 4785, 4365, 1454, 5137).
4. **Unchanged titles fail the same way.** 14 of 39 are in different words (3049, 4909, 20107, 28057, 867).
