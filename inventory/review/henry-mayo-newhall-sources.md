## Summary

* **Living or deceased: DECEASED (1882).** Born 1825-05-13 (most later sources; fits the age in the 1882 death notice) or 1825-05-23 (four 1882 newspapers), Saugus, Massachusetts [conflicting (NEEDS_VERIFICATION; C1)]. Died 1882-03-13 (Monday), San Francisco (his home, 1299 Van Ness Avenue), aged 56 [agreed]. Burial: Laurel Hill Cemetery, San Francisco, Wed 15 Mar 1882 (funeral at 11 a.m. from 1299 Van Ness Avenue); later moved to Cypress Lawn, Colma [agreed (date of removal NEEDS_VERIFICATION)].
* **Record gap (finding, 2026-10-04): no surviving San Francisco civil death register covers 1 May 1873 – 31 Mar 1882 (SFgenealogy listing, quoted); the April 1882+ volume is coroner cases only.** SFgenealogy's entry for the 'Record of Deaths (aka Mortuary Record of the City and County of San Francisco and Register of Deaths of the City and County of San Francisco), 1865-1904' (https://sfgenealogy.org/doku.php?id=san_francisco_county:offline (fetched 2026-10-04 10:35 PT; copy in visitlist-work/src/)) lists '08 Nov 1865 - 30 Sep 1869, Volume 1 (…) 01 Oct 1869 - 30 Apr 1873, Volume 2 (…) 01 Apr 1882 - 30 Jun 1889, Volume 3 (Coroner Cases Only) and Index' ('(…)' stands for the index and holder notes). Newhall died on 13 Mar 1882, inside the gap, so no civil death record of his death is known to survive, and the cause and age as certified cannot come from that source (C13, C1). Library visit list item V6 is removed (kept visible there). Leads: V6a, the Laurel Hill Cemetery (San Francisco) Registers, held by the Society of California Pioneers (they may also bear on the open date of his removal to Cypress Lawn, C14); V6b, original funeral records, pre-1920, at the SFPL History Center. Whether either records a cause of death is NEEDS_VERIFICATION.
* **Life, from site sources:**
  * **Early years.** Born in Saugus, Massachusetts (May 13 or 23, 1825: C1). Left Saugus at 13 (al1882 and the Daily Alta obituary, 1882) and went to sea; worked at an auction house in Philadelphia ('Myers, Claghorn & Co.' in al1882, 'Myers, Clayborn & Co.' in the Daily Alta obituary, 'Myers, Clayton & Co.' in the SF Chronicle via The Daily Press: C3; that the house was his later partner Claghorn's is inference only), then in the auction business in Nashville until 1849 (al1882).
  * **San Francisco, from July 1850.** Arrived on 6 July 1850 (both 1882 obituaries: C2). Briefly in the mines, then an auctioneer: with Hall & Martin's house on Jackson Street (1850 directory), then, by the firm's notices, seven stages under six names: Newhall & Hall (by April 1852), Newhall, Gregory & Co. (by Aug 1854), Newhall, Gregory & Claghorn (in 1854, to 1 Jan 1855), Newhall & Gregory (1 Jan 1855 to 1 Jan 1858), Newhall & Co. with E. W. Eddy (from 1 Jan 1858), Newhall, Gregory & Co. again (dissolved 4 Jan 1860) and H.M. Newhall & Co. (from 4 Jan 1860) (C4). J. Oscar Eldridge first appears in parentheses under H. M. Newhall & Co. in the 1862 directory (in 1860 he is listed with 'De Ro & E.', in 1861 with 'Poulterer, De Ro & E.'); the 1874 certificate names him a partner, and he retired in 1879; Gilbert Palache was a book-keeper in 1862–64 and a principal by 1870 (directories; lw2182; the 1874 and 1879 clippings).
  * **Railroads.** Became interested in the S.F. & S.J. Railroad in 1865 and was its president for four years until its sale to SP in 1870 (al1882; the Daily Alta obituary; al1870); later an SP director per later sources (C6).
  * **Land.** Bought ranchos 1872–75, about 143,000 acres in all (C7), including Rancho San Francisco on Jan 15, 1875, for $90,000 by private deed (C8). Deeded the 1876 townsite of Newhall to SP's development arm (sg19470102perkins).
  * **Family.** Married Sarah Ann White (d. 1858; sons Henry Gregory, William Mayo, Edwin White) and then her sister Margaret Jane White (sons Walter Scott, George Almer) (lw3325; the 1882 will).
  * **Death.** Thrown from his horse on the ranch in late February or early March 1882 (the day is NEEDS_VERIFICATION); died at his San Francisco home, 1299 Van Ness Avenue, about 7 a.m. on Monday, March 13, 1882, aged 56, of erysipelas or of blood-poisoning following the fall (the 1882 papers differ); funeral at 11 a.m. and burial at Laurel Hill on Wednesday the 15th; later moved to Cypress Lawn (C13, C14).

## Method: reading status of quotes from page images

Nathan's rule (approved 2026-10-04 08:50 PT; level names as revised 09:17 PT; applies from now on): a first machine reading (OCR, or a first image reading by a browser session) is a lead, never a quotation. Quote a page image only from a reading confirmed at full zoom. Every record in section 5.1 has a reading status, so a later session knows which quotes carry that weight. 'Confirmed at full zoom' is given only where we opened a zoomed screenshot with the Read tool and could read the quoted words at letter level; the screenshot is named. Copies of every screenshot used are in newhall-hmn-work/evidence_zoom_20261004/ (original paths and SHA-256 in its manifest.json). Quotes from live scvhistory.com pages and other born-digital text (sections 1–3 and 6) are exact text, not readings of an image, and carry the separate label 'exact text (born-digital)'; where a site page is itself a transcription of an old document (al1882, sfexaminer18820321hmn, lw3325's Daily Alta passage), its quotes are exact for the site page, not for the original print. Browser-transcription rule (Nathan, 2026-10-04 11:07 PT; applied in zm3): a typed transcription from a browser session is not even a lead (such sessions invent and silently correct: an added 'San', a dropped 'of of', a tidied misprint). Only a crop the reviewer has read counts. Any record whose quote came from a browser transcription and not a crop gets no level above 'not read'. ## 3. Conflicts

### C1. Birth date: May 13 or May 23, 1825

* al1882 (California Spirit of the Times, 18 Mar 1882): 'born in Saugus, Mass., May 23, 1825'; webmaster note 1: 'commonly listed as May 13, 1825'
* ap1335, ap1335a, rn7301, reynolds part37, cocsilverspur041115, lw3325 census: May 13, 1825
* lw3231 (1949): 'in May 1825'
* **Outside:** Wikipedia, Wikidata (P569, 1 reference), Find a Grave (snippet): May 13, 1825. 1882 papers on CDNC (section 5.1): Sacramento Daily Union, 14 Mar ('was born May 23, 1825') and the SF Chronicle (The Daily Press, 18 Mar: 'born May 23, 1825'); Daily Alta death notice, 14 Mar: 'aged 56 years and 10 months'; Alameda Encinal, 15 Mar: 'aged 56 years'. Daily Alta obituary, 14 Mar 1882 p. 1 (section 5.1 R10): 'He was born in Saugus, Massachu-setts, May 23d, 1825.' (confirmed at full zoom, 'May' included: the user's full-zoom browser crop DAC18820314.2.9_2, 2026-10-04 ~12:15 PT). C1 stays a recorded conflict; the confirmed 'May' does not resolve it. The death notice's 'aged 56 years and 10 months' is confirmed at full zoom (R9).
* **Evidence favors:** Unresolved. May 23 is in four 1882 papers (al1882, the Daily Alta obituary of 14 Mar, the Sacramento Daily Union and the SF Chronicle). al1882 and the Alta obituary share much wording (C4), and whether any of the four are independent is NEEDS_VERIFICATION; C1 is not settled by counting them. May 13 is the usual later date and fits the Daily Alta death notice of 14 Mar 1882: 'aged 56 years and 10 months' is exact for May 13, while May 23 gives 56 years, 9 months, 18 days. The notice gives no day count and family notices can round, so it is evidence for May 13, not proof. Not in the printed Vital Records of Saugus to 1849 (1907), births p. 19 (HathiTrust, read 2026-10-04).
* **Would settle it (where held):** Saugus town clerk's birth register (original; the 1907 printed volume omits him); his gravestone at Cypress Lawn (Section I, Lot 347 per Find a Grave; inscription not transcribed on lw3325); the Laurel Hill Cemetery registers (Society of California Pioneers; library visit list V6a), if they record age (NEEDS_VERIFICATION; no San Francisco civil death register survives for March 1882: record gap, Summary); Cypress Lawn interment record (Cypress Lawn office).

### C2. When he reached California

* al1882: 'until 1849, when he came to California July 6th, 1850'
* cocsilverspur041115: 'He arrived in California on July 5, 1850, after sailing from New Orleans and crossing the Panama isthmus'
* hmn-bio-foundation: 'reached San Francisco in July of 1850' after six months ill in Panama
* the 2012 essay ×7: 'Henry didn't arrive in 1849. He arrived in 1850'
* lw3231 (1949 program): 'came to California in the winter of 1849-1850'
* ap1335 ×3: 'sought his fortune in the California Gold Rush of 1849' (a framing, not an arrival date)
* **Outside:** Wikipedia: arrived 1850 after six months in Panama. Daily Alta obituary, 14 Mar 1882 p. 1 (section 5.1 R10): 'arrived here, after considerable delay at the Isthmus of Panama, July 6th, 1850' ('July 6th, 1850' confirmed at full zoom).
* **Evidence favors:** July 1850 (the 1882 obituary, the Foundation, the 2012 essay and the 2015 documentary agree on the month). The day: 6 July in both 1882 obituaries (al1882 'July 6th, 1850'; the Daily Alta of 14 Mar 1882 'July 6th, 1850', confirmed at full zoom). cocsilverspur041115's 'July 5' differs by a day; it is not listed as a live error, because the passenger lists would decide (settle).
* **Would settle it (where held):** Daily Alta California, 5–8 July 1850, steamer arrivals and passenger lists (CDNC); Rasmussen, San Francisco Ship Passenger Lists (book).
