## The #28055 correction

**What was done.** On 7 October #28055 was retitled "John Lang, in Pen Pictures from the Garden of the World". Its originallyPublishedTitle holds "Pen Pictures From the Garden of the World: An Illustrated History of Los Angeles County, Calif."

**Where that came from.** Leon's citation on the legacy pages, the record titles and the file name "penpictureslacounty". Not the scan.

**What the scan shows.**
- The scan (asset #20098, legacy/penpictureslacounty_large.jpg; on Reggie gif/penpictureslacounty_large.jpg) is the title page of "AN ILLUSTRATED HISTORY OF LOS ANGELES COUNTY CALIFORNIA ... CHICAGO: THE LEWIS PUBLISHING COMPANY. 1889."
- "Pen Pictures" is not printed on it.
- Archive.org's catalogue lists "Pen pictures from the garden of the world" as a different book: Santa Clara County, Lewis, 1888.

**Proposed correction:**
- title: **"John Lang, in An Illustrated History of Los Angeles County"**;
- originallyPublishedTitle: **"An Illustrated History of Los Angeles County, California"**, as the title page prints it;
- Leon's citation stays as he wrote it, on his pages and in legacyHeadline.

**Not checked:** whether "Pen Pictures" is printed anywhere else in the Los Angeles volume, on the spine or the cover. If it is, Leon's name for the book has a source and the correction is smaller.

**The same misnaming probably sits on:**
- the other penpictures_* pages: penpictures_johnlang, penpictures_rdelvalle, penpictures_ydelvalle, needham-penpictures;
- any footnote that cites "Pen Pictures 1889" (Ygnacio del Valle's among them).

Those are listed and not changed.
