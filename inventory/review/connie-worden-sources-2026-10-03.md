# Connie Worden: every source the archive and the mirror hold (3 October 2026)

Read-only research by Claude for Nathan. Nothing was written to the database. Scope: person #16418 in Craft, the Reggie mirror (`/Volumes/Reggie/SCVHistory/scvhistory.com`), and the repo's inventory and docs. The aim is to show what a full profile on the Mentry pattern (docs/PROFILES.md, "The shape that worked") could draw on.

Names she appears under: Connie Worden (to about 1990), Connie Worden-Roberts / Connie Worden Roberts (from her second marriage on), "Mrs. Worden" (1975 press), "Constance Alice Batterman" (birth name, obituary only). No "Connie Roberts" or "Constance Worden" anywhere in the mirror.

**A search trap worth logging in ERRORLOG.md.** In this shell `grep` is a wrapper around `ugrep -I`, which skips any file it thinks is binary. Most legacy pages are ISO-8859-1, so they are skipped without warning: a plain `grep -rli "Connie Worden"` over the mirror returned 50 files; `/usr/bin/grep -rla` with `LC_ALL=C` returned 207, including her own obituary. Earlier mirror sweeps run with plain `grep` may have missed pages in the same way.

## 4. Cityhood

The sources agree on these points:
- **Canyon County, 1976-77.** Co-chair with Dan Hon (Reynolds 69; Simmons 1976, contemporary; CW9901, her own account; Woman of the Year caption 1975). Her account: petitions "collecting 25 percent of the registered voters in a 250-square-mile area"; the governor's commission under Donald Hagman; local voters about 60 percent in favor; the rest of the county 4 to 1 against.
- **Cityhood Feasibility Subcommittee, 1985.** Spokesperson (press kit, October 14, 1985; Signal, November 1, 1985).
- **City of Santa Clarita Formation Committee, 1985-87.** Vice chair under Garasi, Boyer and Donnelly in turn (Reynolds 70; LW3071; LW3060). The fourth signer of Petition No. 1 (LW2612). Spokesperson at the first public forum, January 1987 (gt8702). Collected 2,000 of the 24,000 signatures herself (her 2007 piece; KHTS 2014). Negotiated with LAFCO and the supervisors (formal obituary).
- **After incorporation.** First Planning Commission, 1988 to 1990 (citycommissioners.htm).

One conflict to keep, not resolve: her 2007 piece says "24,000 signatures" and "25 percent," while her own 1999 account gives the 25 percent figure for Canyon County. Minor, and nothing contradicts it.

## 5. Historical Society and preservation (only what sources show)

- **SCV Historical Society:**
  - Donor: $25 toward the Pardee House roof (minutes, April 22, 1996); $100 (deposit, September 28, 2001).
  - May 19, 2003: she came to the board meeting as a guest, with Leon Worden presiding as president. She proposed that the Society form a foundation and laid out its first annual fundraiser in a signed report ("Respectfully submitted, Connie Worden-Roberts Member"). The board tabled the idea; she was to meet with Sue Yurosek and Mary Ann Colf on June 12. No source found shows a Society office or board seat.
  - Reynolds' History, published by the Society: she proofread it and reworked the county and city formation chapters (Preface); her comments "are in the archives of the Santa Clarita Valley Historical Society" (Epilogue).
  - A memorial celebration for her was held "at the Historical Center" before her death (Harte obituary, 2015, quoted speaker).
- **Friends of Hart Park:** a founding director in 1981 (bylaws; 1986 resolution); still a member at her death (formal obituary).
- **Placerita Canyon Nature Center Associates:** member (formal obituary; KHTS 2014).
- **Archaeology:** she studied archaeology at San Jose State (obituary) and was on the 1970 UCLA and CSUN Elderberry Canyon dig with her son (lw2597a, Leon Worden's retrospective note). Artifacts from that dig are displayed at the Society's museum, but the page does not say who gave them.
