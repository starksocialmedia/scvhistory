## The finding in one paragraph

For school and college boards, no one is appointed when the nominees do not outnumber the seats. Education Code 5326 sends the case to section 5328. Section 5328 says the nominee "shall be seated at the organizational meeting of the board". The governing board appoints only "if no person has been nominated or if an insufficient number is nominated", and only those appointees are seated "as if elected". Messina was a nominee in 2013, so he was seated, not appointed. Nathan is right. The election was cancelled, though, so he was not elected at a held election that year either. The verb "appoint" is correct only where the governing statute uses it: a special district (Elections Code 10515: the supervising authority "shall make these appointments"; Plambeck, 2011), a city (Elections Code 10229(a)(1): "Appoint to the office the person who has been nominated"; Gibbs, 2024), and a board filling a vacancy or a shortfall of nominees (Hall, 2013).

## How the misreading arose

1. The County titles every list "Appointment in Lieu of Election Due to Insufficiency of Candidates", for every kind of body. The lists were read as saying that each named person was appointed.
2. fix_sole_candidate_terms_2026_10_04.php (4 October) set one wording for all of them, "Appointed as the sole candidate; no election held", on the ground that "This follows the statute's verb ('appoint')". That verb belongs to Elections Code 10515 (special districts). The same script moved the school footnotes' citation from 10515 to Education Code 5326 and 5328 but kept 10515's verb. Section 5328's verb for a nominee is "seated".
3. The 5328 quotation in every school footnote, "shall be seated at the organizational meeting of the board ... as if elected at a district election", splices two clauses. "As if elected" belongs to the clause about persons the board appoints when too few were nominated.
4. The same script carried the verb forward to later terms on its own reading ("again: appointed as the sole candidate"): Pearson 2018 and 2022, Weinstein 2020 and 2024, Solomon 2020, DeFigueiredo 2020 and 2024, MacDonald 2022. create_coc_trustee_terms_2026_10_05.php copied the wording to eight college terms. Three terms (Pearson 2018, Berger 2018, MacDonald 2018) match the person to a seat-only County line by continued service.
5. Two older inference patterns in derive_board_holdings.php (3 October): CEDA's missing contest read as a cancellation (DeFigueiredo 2007), and CEDA's incumbent flag read as "an earlier appointment" (Emmons, Tannehill, Wigdor, Olsen).
6. Whole-tenure records built from "first appointed" phrases (create_office_gap_holdings.php, build_walters_profile.php, create_hart_early_members.php; 3 October) keep "appointed" for 9 to 22 years across elections won with votes: Umeck, Walters, Dinsenbacher, Gary Martin.

The County lists themselves were read correctly: every in-lieu term checked has its contest on the County list, and where the County names nominees, the person is named.
