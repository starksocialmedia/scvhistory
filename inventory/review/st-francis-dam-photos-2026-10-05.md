# St. Francis Dam: the archive's photographs, 5 October 2026

Claude, read-only research for Nathan, a companion to `st-francis-dam-draft-2026-10-05.json`. Nothing was imported, written to Craft or committed.

**Method.** The Reggie mirror was not mounted today. The pages were read from the local latin-1 index of all 33,690 mirror pages built earlier this session (the same index the events inventory used). Photo pages are the mirror's ID-named pages (`/scvhistory/xx0000.htm`) and its photo galleries; "by title" means the page's `<title>` names the dam, which is how Leon labels the series. Groups were assigned by title keyword and then corrected by hand. Craft photographs were matched by `legacyUrl` against all 1,563 photograph records. Counts are of pages, so a view and its enlargement (LW2642g, LW2642gb) count twice.

The events inventory counted about 166 more photo pages that name the dam only in the caption. Most are other subjects whose captions mention the flood (the Harry Carey ranch series, Callahan's Old West, the aqueduct). Of those, these are in Craft and bear on the event:

- **#4177, LW2579**, "Location of Estancia & Milk House": "Detail from panoramic photograph of Castaic Junction on March 18, 1928, showing flood damage from the St. Francis Dam. Published in Charles Outland's 'Man-Made Disaster' (1963/77)." A real photograph of the valley's damage, but a book page with printed labels and arrows naming the milk house and the "asistencia" (which Leon doubts). Its image exists in archiveMedia (assets 1760, 10975) but is not attached to the record.
- **#3149, LW2271**, the Photoplay page of May 1928: the Carey ranch "destroyed by the breaking of the St. Francis Dam", and Hart's ranch home "used as a center for relief work." A contemporary report, and a magazine page rather than a photograph of the flood. Image assets 14044 and 10499 exist, not attached.
- The Carey ranch series (#2857 LW2135, #2779 LW2087, #2763 LW2071, #2951 LW2167, #3589 LW2409, #2839 LW2124, #2869 LW2142 and its parts, #3157 LW2278, #5651 LW3794): photographs of the ranch and trading post before 1928 or of the site in 2001, whose captions say the trading post was washed away. They are evidence for the Carey ranch record, not pictures of the event. **Do not link them to the event.**
- Pages that only mention the dam in passing (aqueduct construction, Callahan's Old West, Elks Lodge tokens, the 1938 flood, the Dry Canyon house): not related.

No other dam photograph is in Craft. `photoEvents` is used by no photograph in Craft.

## Recommendation for the record

Link three existing photographs, each with the tie its own caption makes:

1. **#28063 (LW2054)**, William Mulholland, 1924: "St. Francis Dam Builder" (footnote 2).
2. **#4177 (LW2579)**, Castaic Junction on March 18, 1928 (footnote 8).
3. **#3149 (LW2271)**, Photoplay, May 1928 (footnotes 7 and 14).

**Band image: leave it empty until the first import.** None of the three is a band: LW2054 is an upright portrait (and a band of the builder's face would say something the record does not), LW2579 carries a book's labels and arrows, LW2271 is a magazine page. A crop of LW2579 below its arrows would be an edited image and would need your word with the edit recorded; it is not recommended while better originals wait in the mirror.

**First choice once imported: LW3083**, "Ruins of St. Francis Dam & Empty Reservoir, March 1928", a George Watson photograph from an original 4x5 negative Leon bought from the Watson Archive in 2017, so its rights are the clearest of the series. Second: **LW3703**, an original Associated Press wirephoto Leon bought in 2020, San Francisquito Creek running with the floodwaters on March 13, 1928, which is the valley's own view of the day.

These carry paragraph 2 of the body.

Before any import: the content advisory goes on every source record about the dead (DATA-MODEL, Content advisories), and each photograph's credit line decides whether the image can be shown (several are "online image only", "used by permission", or a library's).
