# Hart district Governing Board, Trustee Area 2: November 8, 2022 General Election

Researched 2026-10-03 PT (Grok Bot, read-only). All URLs below were fetched on 2026-10-03 PT unless marked otherwise. No repo was modified.

## Answer

Trustee Area 2 was on the November 8, 2022 ballot and was contested. It was not cancelled or appointed in lieu of election. The Los Angeles County RR/CC Final Official Statement of Votes Cast (election 4300, page 169 of 430, contest "WM S. HART UNION HIGH SCH / GOV BD MEMBER TR AREA 2") reports registration 36,496, ballots cast 21,104, BOB JENSEN 11,639 and ANDREW TABAN 5,736. The RR/CC text results page shows the same totals as 66.99% and 33.01% (results as of 12/05/2022 12:15:21). Incumbent Bob Jensen was re-elected. Registrar Dean C. Logan certified the results on December 5, 2022, and the Board of Supervisors was scheduled to declare the election concluded on December 6, 2022. The district's board page lists Bob Jensen, CPA, as the Trustee Area No. 2 representative with a "current term 2022 - 2026". Areas 2, 3 and 5 were all on the 2022 ballot. Our election data on the box has no Hart district contest records for 2022 at all, so this is a gap in our data and not a missing election.
