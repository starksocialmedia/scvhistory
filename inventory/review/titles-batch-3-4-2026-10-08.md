# Titles against the scans, batches 3 and 4 of 4 (8 October 2026)

For Nathan's read, one at a time. Nothing written. Prepared overnight by Claude, read-only. The dry run is scripts/import/titles_batch_3_4_2026_10_08.php ($APPLY = false; run once as a dry run, it matched all 27 "Holds" titles below and wrote nothing). Edit it to your rulings before any apply.

The rule and conventions are batch 1's (titles-batch-1-2026-10-07.md), with your batch 1 and 2 rulings applied: advertising copy is not a title (#5541); a publisher or agency name belongs in fields, not after a colon (#26573); a serial or one-word head can stand alone (#4735 "Ramona"); an event's name is the title, not a theme line (#5359); "Menu", "Program Book" and the like name the kind of record, not the title; envelopes, letters and inner pages keep a description.

Every printed line below was read by eye from the scan itself, not from the OCR, the census or a caption. Where a scan was not in the titles-vs-scans previews it was copied from Reggie (/mnt/reggie inside DDEV; Reggie read fine all night) into storage/runtime/titles-vs-scans/b3/. Which scan was read is named where it is not the record's own picture in Craft.

This batch: all 37 left. The 35 ephemera titles shortened or extended, and the two ephemera titles in different words (#4155, #18991). Of the 37, 27 propose a new title and 10 keep the one held. Four are marked **your call**: #4567, #21936, #5481, #20099. One question covers three more: whether a program's printed date goes in its title (#5377, #3191, #4943; see the note after #3191).

Where Leon's headline leaves the title: on a photograph it is already in the catalogue entry (#5377's is empty and gets its page's title tag, as #4909 did); on an article or document it goes to "Headline on SCVHistory.com" (legacyHeadline), from the page's title tag minus the site name.
