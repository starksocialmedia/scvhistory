## How this was done

- Rules applied, from docs/PROFILES.md: a person belongs here for what they did here; count the source set (fewer than three sources from the lifetime of the events means a retrospective profile); Leon Worden's Hart roster is primary but has erred; a note that something was not found records the search; the district's own trustee bio comes before campaign material, and campaign pieces are attributed.
- Public life only. Offices, elections, statements in the role, and profession as given on the ballot or in candidate statements. Family, health, ages, birth dates, addresses and contact details are left out of the recommendations, and each dossier says what was left out. Saved copies of the district page and Smart Voter pages have members' e-mail, telephone and campaign contact blocks redacted; the manifest gives the sha256 of the unredacted original.
- Reused, not redone: `inventory/review/hart-pre1995-terms-2026-10-05.md` for Aliano, Hassel, Olivares, Hanrion, and for King, Dinsenbacher and Warren before 1995.
- "Contemporary" below means written while the events were current; "retrospective" means a later account or a compiled list (the roster, Leon Worden's lists, a memorial).

### Linda Storli #25157: full public-life profile

**Recommendation: full public-life profile.** Two Hart terms, two presidencies, the first Parks Commission and three council runs: a 37-year public record in the valley, with the district's own bio and at least ten contemporary news items.

- Name variants: Linda Storli; Linda H. Storli; Linda Hovis Storli (ballot 2020, 2024); Mrs. Linda Storli (district page); Linda Storli-Koontz (SCVNews, 2015).
- What the archive holds: Person #25157 (no body, no occupation). Hart holdings #28612 (Trustee Area 1, 2015-12 to 2020-12, elected) and #28614 (2020-12 to 2024-12, elected, howEnded expired; she lost on November 5, 2024). Candidacies: Hart Area 1 2015, 2020, 2024; City Council 1987, 1992, 1994. Named in article #26999 ("The ladder that runs the other way").
- Board service as held, and disagreements: Elected 2015 (Area 1, defeating incumbent Mercado-Fortine), re-elected 2020, lost 2024 (third of three, behind Aakash Ahuja and Mercado-Fortine). Officer years: Assistant Clerk 2018, Clerk 2019 and 2023, President 2020 and 2024. No disagreement on the terms. Minor: election-night counts (SCVNews 1,193 to 913) differ from the CEDA canvass (1,428 to 1,078) for 2015.
- Public life in the valley beyond the board:
  - Canyon High School teacher for 30 years (her own statements and press); retired from teaching in 2015.
  - Member of the City of Santa Clarita's first Parks and Recreation Commission (1988 to 1990 on Leon Worden's commissioners list); by her own bio, on the city formation committee of 1987.
  - Stood for the City Council in 1987, 1992 and 1994 (archive candidacies).
  - Realtor (ballot designation "Teacher/Realtor", 2015).
  - Board president for 2020 (the year of COVID-19 closures and the Saugus High shooting remembrances) and for 2024; spoke for the 2021 decision to retire the Hart High Indian mascot and the 2024 reveal of the Hart Hawks.

### Paul Strickland #25425: full public-life profile

**Recommendation: full public-life profile.** Two school boards over 18 years, two Hart presidencies, the Arts Commission chair and an Assembly candidacy; the district release of 2013 is a body-own summary and the 2011-2013 press is contemporary.

- Public life in the valley beyond the board:
  - Trustee of the Sulphur Springs Union School District, 1995 to 1999.

  - Not related to State Senator Tony Strickland (SCVNews, 2011): the 2014 congressional race "Strickland, Knight" is Tony Strickland and is not his.
