## Per person

### Mary P. Bonelli (#28641)

- **Valley**: the Saugus School District's trustee when the high school district formed (roster note). "Gracious lady of the Bonelli Ranch" (1955 yearbook). A Mary Bonelli contributed to the 1964 Republican Women's Club cookbook, and a "Mary P. Bonelli" is on a Saugus Station move donor list in the Historical Society's 1980s minutes book. Name only in both.
- **Board**: 1945-03-09 to 1957 (roster). On every yearbook board 1949 to 1955 and starred in 1952. No disagreement.
- **Key quote**: "Gracious lady of the Bonelli Ranch is an active Board Member, Mrs. Mary P. Bonelli, who was away and could not be photographed casually." (Tomahawk 1955, flipbook page 10, read from the image.)
- **Lead, not stated**: her relation to William G. Bonelli (1895-1970) or his son. No source on the mirror states it.
- **Not found**: dates, spouse, Saugus board years. Searched the mirror for bonelli, "mary p", "mrs. (william|wm|w. g.) bonelli", and wife, mother or widow near Bonelli. LA Times "Bonelli": 50 stories, none about her.

### Charles M. Brown (#28643)

- **Valley**: Mint Canyon and Sulphur Springs trustee in 1945 (roster). "Charles M. Brown, prominent resident of the canyon, and a trustee of the William S. Hart High School District, was selected as the club's first president" (Newhall Signal, 15 January 1948).
- **Board**: 1945 to 1948. He is not on the 1949 yearbook board. "**Mr. Charles Brown" in the September 1952 program: he had died by then.
- **Count**: one lifetime source, so a retrospective profile. The middle initial rests on the 1948 Signal alone.
- **Not found**: dates and occupation. The mirror's other Charles Browns are a different man (the Signal founder, died 1923) and students.

### S. S. Donaldson (#28645)

- **Valley**: Newhall School District trustee in 1945 (roster; Worden 1997). Gave "the address of welcome" at the Legion gold star citation program (Signal, 16 May 1946). Listed as "Donaldson S S So Calif Gas Co" in the 1958 directory.
- **Board**: 1945 to 1953. Secretary in the 1951 yearbook and "President of the Board" in the 1952 yearbook.
- **Disagreement**: he is unstarred in the September 1952 program, while Hadley is starred though the roster seats Hadley only in 1953. This may mean Donaldson had left by then, or only that he was absent that night. Footnote both and state neither.
- **Lead, not stated**: Bill Rice's memoir names Gas Co. neighbours "the Donaldson family, Stu and Mary". The Gas Co. link fits, but no source joins "Stu" to S.S. Keep the initials.

### Thomas M. Frew Jr., also Tom III (#28647)

- **Life**: born 31 January 1895 and died 17 August 1963 (Worden, AP0724). Ruth Newhall (Gazette, December 1996) gives 1896. Footnote both; neither cites a document.
- **Valley**: came to Newhall in 1900 as a child. He was a World War I horseshoer and ran T.M. Frew & Son, the blacksmith shop on Spruce Street, after his father. He converted it to a welding and machine shop in 1924 (City staff report, 2011).
  - Newhall Chamber of Commerce in 1923: nominated for office and on the Juvenile Welfare committee (minutes).
  - A trustee of the Legion's theater corporation in 1940 (Crowl, retrospective).
  - A source for Brunner's 1940 Newhall School history.
  - Among the sellers of the Frew land added to Hart Park in 1958 ("from Thomas M, Frew, Jr,, et al", County notice).
  - A long-time Newhall School trustee and its president (roster note; Worden, retrospective).
- **Board**: first president, 28 March 1945 (1952 program, quoting the minutes). Resigned 1951 ("MR. T. M . FREW (resigned)", 1951 yearbook).
- **Error noted**: Worden's AP1535 puts "Tom Frew II" on the Newhall School Board in 1936. His own AP0724 has Tom II dying in 1928, so the 1936 trustee was probably Tom III. Do not repeat it.
- **Count**: ten lifetime sources, so a full profile.

### Mildred M. Gilmour (#28649)

- **Valley**: the Castaic School District's trustee in 1945 (roster). A grandmother by 1955 (yearbook caption).
- **Board**: 1945 to 1956. First board secretary, 28 March 1945 (1952 program). President in the 1950 yearbook. On every board 1949 to 1955.
- **Lead, not stated**: a Hasley Canyon Gilmour family (1958 directory), and a gift by Gloria Allen "in memory of Mr. and Mrs. LeRoy Gilmour" (1980s minutes). Gloria Allen and Merle Gilmour are siblings (Merle Gilmour obituary, 2014). No source names Mildred as Mrs. LeRoy Gilmour.

### C. L. (Charles L.) Dillenbeck (#28651)

- **Valley**: "Charles L. Dillenbeck, Lion Tamer", a charter member of the Mint Canyon Lions (Signal, January 1948). Listed as "Dillenbeck CL 23041 W 16th" in the 1958 directory.
- **Board**: 1948 to 1952. On the 1949 yearbook board, so he was seated by 1948-49. President in the 1951 yearbook. "Mr. Charles Dillenbeck" in the 1952 program.
- **Identity question for Nathan**: the roster links C.L. to db4801, Charlie Dillenbeck Sr., whose photograph inscriptions call him "the custodian at Hart High School".
  - A Charles Dillenbeck is on the Hart maintenance staff in the 1953 and 1955 yearbooks, and Joe Kapp's memoir has "Charlie Dillenback" running buildings and grounds.
  - The 1958 directory has two men: CL on 16th Street, and Charlie at Oak Spring Canyon, the address of the market family.
  - The Charlie Dillenbeck on the Sulphur Springs board in 1963 (Signal) is probably the son, the market owner. That is not shown.
  - Stating that the trustee was the custodian rests on Worden's link and the inscription.

### Walter Russell Cook (#28653)

- **Life**: born 26 November 1896 in Riverside; died 9 May 1954 in Newhall (Worden, LW3799, citing the Signal of 13 May 1954).
- **Valley**: arrived January 1920 and married Pearl Walk in Newhall Pass on 20 June 1920 (photographs, NW2001).
  - Ran the Ridge View Dairy at "Cook corner" with Pearl.
  - Kiwanis president 1928 to 1935; Scoutmaster of Troop 2; member of Legion Post 507; Mason.
- **Board**: 1951 to 1954. Clerk in the 1952 yearbook; president in the 1953 yearbook. At the September 1952 dedication he gave the welcome and accepted the auditorium.
- **Correction**: he died in office on 9 May 1954. The holding's end of "1955, unknown" should be 1954-05-09, died in office. The Signal issues Worden cites are not on the mirror; finding them would add lifetime sources.

### Chester A. Allen (#28655)

- **Valley**: the 1950 yearbook advertisement for the Mint Canyon Gem Shop names him: "C. A. ALLEN, Store: 17145 Sierra Highway, Two miles north of Solemint Store", read from the page image. The OCR wrongly joins it to Tom Cox's tire advertisement.
  - The 1955 yearbook shows "Member C. A. Allen ... engaged in lapidary art".
  - Listed at 17211 Sierra Highway, Saugus, in the 1958 directory.
  - Identity of the advertiser with the trustee: medium (same name and the lapidary photograph).
- **Board**: 1952 to 1956. Clerk in the 1953 yearbook.

### Howard Alvin Blackwell (#28657)

- **Valley**: a charter member and short-term director of the Mint Canyon Lions (Signal, January 1948).
- **Board**: 1953 only (roster). He is in the 1953 yearbook board photograph and absent from the September 1952 list.
- **Count**: two lifetime sources, so a retrospective profile, one or two lines.

### Carleton E. Hadley (#28659)

- **Name**: the roster's "Charleton" appears nowhere else. Other sources have Carleton, Carlton or C. E. Nathan to decide the record title.
- **Valley**:
  - Co-owner with R.W. Hogan of H&H, the Firestone store on Spruce Street, bought in 1947 (Worden, citing the Signal of 24 April 1947). The 1955 yearbook mentions "his Firestone store".
  - Chamber president in 1952 (chamber list).
  - Bands and music chairman at the 1948 Fourth of July, and a church music director in the same program.
  - Married Mrs. Pearl Cook, Walter Cook's widow, on 11 December 1968 in Las Vegas (Record-Press, 8 January 1969).
- **Board**: roster 1953 to 1959. Starred in the September 1952 program, so on the board by then: footnote both. President in the 1955 yearbook.

### Julio J. Lombardi (#28661)

- **Life**: married Ida Orsi on 26 November 1938; died 21 December 1986 (Ida Lombardi obituary, 2014).
- **Valley**: a Saugus rancher (Worden, citing the Signal). Co-founder with Gene Doty of the OLPH parish barbecue (Doty obituary; OLPH history 1994). Father of five (1955 yearbook).
- **Board**: Worden (LW3799, citing the Signal of 27 May 1954) says he was the only name on the ballot at the regular election two weeks after Cook's death. He won 302 to 224 over write-ins for Pearl Cook. That is May 1954; the roster starts him in 1955. Propose 1954-05 with both footnoted. He served to 1961 or 1962: he seconded the lease motion of August 1960 and is "Member" in the 1961 yearbook.
- **Not stated**: any link to Lombardi Ranch in Bouquet Canyon.

### Howard (H. H.) Gulley (#28663)

- **Board**: 1956 to 1962. Commencement programs 1958, 1960 and 1961; he moved the lease resolution on 4 August 1960; clerk in the 1961 yearbook. "Board Member Howard Gully presents his daughter, Martha, with her diploma" (1959 yearbook).
- **Lead, not stated**: the Signal obituary of William E. Gulley (24 August 1961) says he homesteaded Hasley Canyon in 1930 "with brothers Cy and Howard". But it lists surviving brothers Cy and Charles, not Howard, though the trustee was serving in 1960 and 1961. No source joins them.

### Ernest Malam (#28665)

- **Board**: 1956 only, and only on the roster. No yearbook or program names him: the 1956 and 1957 yearbooks print no board page.
- **Valley**: Butch Watkins's memoir (retrospective) says Joyce Malam's father "was on the William S. Hart Union High School Board and who also owned Malam's Market in Forrest Park". It gives no first name. Joyce Eileen Malam graduated in 1958 (commencement program). An Ernest Malam is in the 1957 and 1958 directories.
- **Count**: no lifetime source names him as a trustee. One line, retrospective, with the roster flagged as the only source for the term.

### Adrian W. Adams (#28667)

- **Living status**: not established, so public life only.
- **Valley**:
  - Opened a law practice in Newhall on 1 May 1953 (Signal editor's note, 2003). Listed as an attorney on Market Street in the 1958 directory.
  - Chamber president in 1956.
  - A former FBI agent, and on the Newhall Elementary board when Reagan appointed him judge of the Newhall Municipal Court on 27 January 1970, the court's second seat (Signal, 28 January 1970). Retired 1991.
  - Founding chairman of the board that built Henry Mayo Newhall Memorial Hospital.
  - SCV Man of the Year, 1980.
  - Wrote "Tales of the Newhall Court" (The Signal, 12 April 2003).
- **Board**: 1957 to 1962. President in the 1962 yearbook; signed the 1960 lease.
- **Hub**: aa5001.htm and its sidebar, about ten pages, are candidates to import.

### Edith S. Palmer (#28669)

- **Board**: 1957 to 1967. She is on every board page 1958 to 1967. President in the 1960 yearbook ("MRS. EDITH PALMER: President"). Signed the 1960 lease.
- **Valley**: "Mrs. Paul Palmer" (1965 and 1966 yearbooks). A Paul Palmer, attorney, is in the 1957 and 1958 directories. That he is her husband is by name only, so do not state his trade.

### Dr. William D. Ross (#28671)

- **Valley**: Newhall's dentist from 1 September 1933, first in the Newhall Community Hospital building, giving dental talks in the schools (Brunner 1940, "Source of information: Dr. Wm. D. Ross").
  - From 1941 his office was at Sixth and Spruce, 24229 San Fernando Road (photograph 1961; 1958 directory).
  - Pallbearer for Fred W. Trueblood Sr. (Signal, May 1960).
- **Board**: the June 1958 commencement program lists "*Dr. William D. Ross" with "*Member Elect"; the roster starts him in 1959. Propose 1958 with both footnoted. President in the 1961 yearbook; clerk in 1962 and 1964; served through 1968.

### Curtis R. Huntsinger (#28673)

- **Living status**: not established, so public life only.
- **Valley**: an Acton turkey rancher of the Huntsinger Turkey Ranches family. He served earlier on the Saugus elementary board (Signal, 16 September 1970).
- **Board**:
  - President in the 1964, 1965 and 1968 yearbooks; homecoming grand marshal (1965 yearbook).
  - Recalled with Edward Duarte on 15 September 1970, 2,450 to 2,323 (Signal, 16 September 1970). The recall followed the board's reversal, led by Huntsinger, of Superintendent Haan over a Canyon High anti-war petition.
  - The Signal says he was "in the midst of his tenth year", and the 1962 yearbook already lists him, so the start is probably 1961 against the roster's 1962.
  - Propose an end of 1970-09-15.
- **Brother**: Worden calls Peter Huntsinger his brother. Peter's LA Times obituary (4 December 1996) says only "three brothers"; not independent.

### Earl Schmidt (#28675)

- **Life**: born 29 January 1906 in Los Angeles; killed by a falling tree at Donner Lake in 1979. Both rest on his granddaughter Cassandra Skaggs (2017, retrospective).
- **Valley**:
  - Grew up on the 6-S Ranch (today's North Oaks).
  - With his brother opened the 6-S Ranch Airpark in 1946 (Shell Progress, May 1947, lifetime).
  - Later managed the Wayside Honor Rancho hog farm.
  - Founding director of the Castaic Lake Water Agency from 1962 until his death; the Earl Schmidt Filtration Plant is named for him.
- **Board**: 1962 to 1969, resigned. On every yearbook board in those years. Skaggs says 1962 to 1969 but may draw on the roster.

## What was searched

All searches were made on 5 October 2026.

- **Archive database**, read with `ddev craft exec`: each record's fields, its holdings, entries related to it, and a Craft search of entries, assets and categories by surname. Nothing was written.
- **Reggie mirror**: every .htm, .html, .txt, .shtml, .asp and .php file, and every flipbook search/bookText.xml, under /Volumes/Reggie/SCVHistory/scvhistory.com. That is 84,019 files, read with Python as bytes decoded utf-8 or cp1252, with case-insensitive regex. Terms per person are in the JSON (`search_terms`): name, initials and spelling variants, such as Hadley and Charleton, Gulley and Gully, and Huntsinger, Hunsinger and Huntzinger.
  - Every Hart yearbook and commencement program from 1949 to 1971 was read for board pages.
  - Page images were read where the OCR was in doubt: the 1952 program p. 2, the 1955 yearbook p. 10, and the 1950 yearbook p. 77.
- **LA Times**: latimes.com site search, oldest first, five result pages per query; each archive story was fetched and its articleBody searched for the name together with a local place word.
  - Queries: Julio Lombardi, Adrian Adams, Bonelli, Malam, Gulley, Curtis Huntsinger, Huntsinger, Edith Palmer, Hadley, Dillenbeck, Gilmour, Blackwell Newhall, Chester Allen, Frew, Donaldson Newhall, Ross dentist Newhall, Earl Schmidt.
  - No story about any of the 18 was found. The hits were other people: water polo players, a hockey player, an Irvine police sergeant, Peter F. Huntsinger. The online archive begins in 1985, so it cannot reach most of these lives.
- **Not searched**: the Signal for 1945 to 1970 (not online), Hart board minutes, newspapers.com, ProQuest, county death indexes, and the SCVTV "Legacy" video of Adams.
