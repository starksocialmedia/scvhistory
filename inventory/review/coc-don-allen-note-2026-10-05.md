5 October 2026. Drafted by Claude Code. Sent by Nathan; the archive does not write to outside bodies.

Subject: A trustee missing from the district's board history: Don Allen, 1973 to about 1975

Hello,

I edit SCVHistory.com, the Santa Clarita Valley's history archive. While recording the district's trustees from 1967, we
found one who does not appear in the district's history of its governing board: Don Allen.

Two independent sources record his service:

1. The Canyon Call, the college's student newspaper, Vol. III, No. 13, May 1, 1973, "Don Allen Elected to Board; Bruce
   Fortine Wins 2d Term": Bruce Fortine "was reelected to a four-year term ... and Don Allen, a COC student, was voted a board
   member for the first time in recent school district elections." Don Allen had stood in the special election of June
   1972 and lost to Francis Claffey.

2. Carl Boyer, Santa Clarita (2015), chapter 4: at the dedication of April 22, 1974, "The other governing board members were
   Peter Huntsinger, Fran Claffey and Don Allen," with Ed Muhl and Boyer himself; and "Later Lou Reiter and Jim Rentz
   succeeded Ed Muhl and Don Allen."

The Canyon Call of February 1975 ("Thirteen Seek Trustee Seats") reports that the election of March 4, 1975 was for three
expiring positions "and an additional unexpired term", which we take to be the remainder of his term after he left the
board; we have not found the date or the reason he left.

Your board history lists John Hackney through 1973 and four members for 1974-1975, so Don Allen's year or so on the board
falls between its lines. Both sources can be read on our site: the Canyon Call volumes at
https://scvhistory.com/scvhistory/files/coccanyoncall_fy19721973/ and Carl Boyer's chapter at
https://scvhistory.com/scvhistory/boyer2015ch04.pdf. His record in our archive gives the sources in full.

With thanks for the history you keep,

Nathan Imhoff
SCVHistory.com
