## Per person

### George Aliano: found

**Appointed May 1994** to the seat Peter Warren vacated. The four other trustees chose him from eight applicants they interviewed on the weekend of 14 and 15 May 1994, and his swearing-in was set for Wednesday, 18 May 1994. The trustees chose an appointment over a special election.

- Quote: "George Aliano was chosen from eight applicants interviewed this weekend by the four other board members to fill the William S. Hart Union High School District trustee post that Peter Warren vacated in March. ... Aliano is scheduled to be sworn in Wednesday. ... Trustees opted to fill the seat by appointment rather than pay for a special election."
  Source: Douglas Alger, "Santa Clarita / Antelope Valley : Aliano to Join Hart School Board," Los Angeles Times, May 17, 1994, https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1994-05-17-me-58785-story.html. Confidence: high for the appointment and the month. Medium for 18 May 1994 as the day he took his seat, because the article gives the date it was scheduled and does not report that it happened.
- Roster: "George Aliano (appointed to complete Warren's unexpired term)" (Leon Worden, William S. Hart Union High School District Governing Board Members, 1945 to Date, SCVHistory.com, /scvhistory/hartschoolboardmembers.htm).
- Consistent: "All four board members said they support uniforms" (Mark Sabbatini, "Board to Weigh Plan for Student Uniforms," Los Angeles Times, May 11, 1994) and "at least three of the four trustees" (Sabbatini, May 18, 1994). Both show the board one member short until mid-May.
- Consistent: CEDA 1995 has Aliano as the incumbent in the short-term contest (the remainder of a vacant term), with 4,659 of 7,906 votes and the ballot designation "Police Captain". This is the election that follows an appointment.
- **1995 to 1997**: elected to the short term on 7 November 1995. The archive already holds this as #28604.
- **End, 1997**: the roster says "did not seek reelection in 1997". CEDA 1997 and the Times of 2 and 6 November 1997 list four candidates, and Aliano is not among them. The Times of 4 and 5 November 1997 (Dade Hayes) is wrong: it calls him an incumbent candidate, then a defeated incumbent. Holding #28604 has howEnded expired, which is right.
- Lead, not stated: the Times of June 1, 1996 quotes "Capt. George Aliano" of the LAPD Foothill Division. The 1994 article calls the trustee "a Los Angeles police officer", so this is probably the same man, but no source connects the two. A George Aliano in 1985 and 1986 prep sports (Saugus High) was a student at the time, so he is not the trustee.

### John Hassel: partly found

**Elected 1991 and seated December 1991.** An independent source confirms the start year. The 1991 return was not found.

- Roster: "John R. Hassel (elected 1991)", on the board of 12-9-1991.
- Independent: "John Hassel served two terms on the William S. Hart Union High School District Governing Board from 1991 - 1999." (Santa Clarita Valley Facilities Foundation, *Creating Value: Story of a Public-Private Partnership*, Point of View Communications, August 20, 2014, "The Passing of a School Board Legend," p. 18, SCVHistory.com, /scvhistory/scvff082014.htm; mirror PDF /scvhistory/files/scvff082014/scvff082014.pdf). The PDF prints an en dash between the years; a hyphen is used here. This is a memorial written after his death, so it is retrospective. It is independent of the roster: it cites neither the roster nor Reynolds or Perkins.
- Contemporary: "said trustee John Hassel" (Douglas Alger, "Schools' Plan Would Let Bible Study Groups Meet in Classrooms," Los Angeles Times, June 18, 1993) and "said board President John Hassel" (Mark Sabbatini, "District Considers Campus Cutbacks, Layoffs," Los Angeles Times, December 16, 1994). The second matches the roster's 1994-95 presidency.
- Election date: the general district election of November 5, 1991 ("the Nov. 5 ballot": Tracey Kaplan, "1 of 2 School Bond Measures Taken Off Ballot," Los Angeles Times, July 25, 1991). No source found names Hassel on that ballot, or gives his votes or placing.
- Confidence: high for service from December 1991 to 1995. The election details were not found.

### Paula Olivares: partly found

**1991 start: roster only.** Service in 1994, and her presidency in 1993-94, are confirmed independently.

- Roster: "Paula E. Olivares, Asst. Clerk (elected 1991)".
- "Paula Olivares, president of Hart's board of trustees" (Mark Sabbatini, "Board OKs Program Requiring Students to Wear Uniforms," Los Angeles Times, June 10, 1994). Also "Board President Paula Olivares" (Sabbatini, "Survey Ordered on Valedictorian Policy," Los Angeles Times, August 12, 1994). Both match the roster, which makes her President for 12-8-1993 to 11-30-1994.
- CEDA 1995: incumbent, ballot designation "Governing Board Member".
- Confidence: medium for the 1991 start, which rests on one source but agrees with everything else. High for her service by June 1994.
- Not a real disagreement: the OCR of the Bowman 1996 yearbook caption reads "Front (I tor) Mrs. Hanrion. Mr. William Dinsenbachcr. President Ms. Paula Olivares." The roster makes Dinsenbacher president in 1995-96. The OCR punctuation may have moved "President" off his name, and the caption falls after 1995 in any case.

### Patricia Hanrion: found

**Elected November 2, 1993. She held no appointment before that.**

- Quote: "William S. Hart Union High School District 3 Elected 100% Precincts Reporting: votes (%) Patricia Hanrion: 9,794 (22%) Peter C. Warren: 9,126 (21%) W. Dinsenbacher*: 8,943 (20%) Sandra L. Loberg*: 8,506 (19%) Dennis V. King*: 7,934 (18%)" ("Final Election Returns," Los Angeles Times, November 4, 1993, https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1993-11-04-me-53120-story.html). She placed first of five candidates for three seats at large.
- "Warren and fellow newcomer Patricia Hanrion toppled two incumbents" (Douglas Alger, "Election Wrapup : Conservatives Gain Board Seats in Santa Clarita School Districts," Los Angeles Times, November 4, 1993).
- Candidacy: "challenged by parent Patricia Hanrion and Peter Warren" (Jonathan Gaw, "Lists of Candidates Finalized in 5 School Board Elections," Los Angeles Times, August 12, 1993).
- Seated: the roster lists "Patricia A. Hanrion (elected 1993)" on the board of 12-8-1993. The Times calls her "Trustee Patricia Hanrion" on March 11, 1994.
- Her own statements: "Wm S. Hart Union H.S. Board Member since 1993" (her candidate page, Smart Voter, League of Women Voters of California, November 6, 2001 election, read in the Wayback Machine's copy of 15 December 2001, http://web.archive.org/web/20011215100241/http://www.smartvoter.org:80/2001/11/06/ca/la/vote/hanrion_p/). Also "when elected in 1993" (her "Goals and Philosophy," Smart Voter, November 8, 2005 election, Wayback copy of 17 May 2008). Both were supplied by the candidate and not checked by the League.
- Before 1993 she appears only as "vice president of the district's advisory council" (Kaplan, Los Angeles Times, July 25, 1991). That is not board service.
- Confidence: high. Holding #28616 is right. It needs this footnote and an election record for November 2, 1993.
- The 1997 contradiction in the roster: it says "did not seek reelection in 1997" on the 1996-97 board and "reelected 1997" on the 1997-98 board. CEDA and the Times final returns settle it: she was reelected. Their vote counts differ:
  - CEDA: 5,102, second (Mercado 5,379).
  - Los Angeles Times, November 6, 1997: 4,780 (28%), second (Mercado 5,037).

  The Times printed election-night figures; CEDA probably has the canvass.

## Other trustees, 1985 to 1995

**Missing from the archive: none.** Every trustee the roster puts on the board between 1985 and 1995 has a person record and a Hart holding: McKeon #26978, Keysor #28797, Stroup #28799, Heidt #28795, Loberg #28801, King #28590 and #28592, Dinsenbacher #28803, Warren #28805, and the four above. Student board members are not counted.

The Times contradicts or adds to the roster in these places:

- **Peter Warren's resignation.** The roster says "elected 1993, resigned 4-6-1994". The Times says "Peter Warren vacated in March" and "Warren, 36, resigned in March, 105 days after he was elected" (Alger, May 17, 1994). Counted from the election on November 2, 105 days is February 15, 1994. Counted from the board's first meeting on December 8, it is March 23, 1994. So the roster says April 6 and the Times says March. Holding #28805 should footnote both and state neither. Warren was elected second of five, with 9,126 votes (21%).
- **Loberg and King, 1993.** The roster says each "did not seek reelection in 1993". The Times says they ran and lost: "All three trustees for the William S. Hart Union High School District have filed: William Dinsenbacher, Dennis King and Sandra Loberg" (Jack Cheevers and John Chandler, "Deadlines Extended for Council, Board Races," Los Angeles Times, August 7, 1993). The returns put Loberg fourth and King fifth for three seats. Holdings #28801 and #28592 say howEnded expired; by the Times it is defeated. Like the Hanrion note of 1997, this roster note is wrong, and it is the second error of that kind.
- **1989, uncontested.** "There is no contest this year in the district, where incumbents William S. Dinsenbacher, Sandra L. Loberg and Dennis V. King are running unopposed" (Steve Padilla, "Santa Clarita Valley School Races Are Low-Key," Los Angeles Times, October 21, 1989; also Stephanie Chavez and Leslie Berger, August 12, 1989). The sources do not show whether the County held the vote or seated them without one. The archive has no 1989 election record.
- **1985.** The Times returns are McKeon* 1,947, Loberg* 1,598 and King 1,395 elected; Thompson 1,311, Dinsenbacher 1,056, Wire 858 and Grade 387 lost ("Complete Returns From Valley Area : School Boards," Los Angeles Times, November 7, 1985). The filing story names only McKeon and Loberg as incumbents for the three seats (Herbert A. Sample, August 15, 1985), so Keysor did not run. Holding #28797 has howEnded unknown; it could read "did not run". That is an inference from the candidate list: medium.
- **Agrees with the roster.**
  - Loberg as board president in February and March 1989 and in October 1993 (Padilla; Alger).
  - The 1987-88 board in the Hart High Class of 1988 commencement program, p. 4: "Mr. Dennis King, President Mrs. Sandra L. Loberg, Clerk Ms. Clara J. Stroup, Ass't. Clerk Mr. Wm. S. Dinsenbacher, Member Mr. Gerald H. Heidt, Member".
  - Dinsenbacher re-elected in 1993, third with 8,943 votes.
- **Stroup and Heidt.** The roster lists both on the 1990-91 board for the last time. No source found says whether they ran in 1991, so howEnded unknown stays unknown.

  - The 1997 board.htm is placeholder "SAMPLE DATA".

- **Archive database**, read with `ddev craft exec`: there is no Hart election record before November 7, 1995.

  - The LA County Registrar-Recorder's past results for 1991 and 1993 (not checked in this pass)
  - newspapers.com and ProQuest (no access)
