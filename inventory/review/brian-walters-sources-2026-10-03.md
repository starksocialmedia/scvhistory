## Conflicts with the election records

- None in the outcomes. CEDA and the archive have 2013 Elected and 2022 Not elected, and the news agrees.
- **Missing service record.** There is no `officeHoldings` row, yet he held office from December 2009 to December 2022. Service began by appointment, not election, and he was seated again in 2018 without a ballot contest. A record built only from the candidacies would understate his tenure.
- **The 2018 term is absent from CEDA** because the election was cancelled (appointment in lieu). The district header "Current Term: 2018 - 2022" and the Signal article fill the gap.
- **2009 to 2013 is not fully explained.** The district's 2009 release says the appointee would serve "until the next regularly scheduled election which will occur in November 2011." CEDA has no Newhall rows for 2011. KHTS (2013) calls 2013 a "second consecutive term after re-election", which suggests one earlier term. Either the 2011 contest for the seat was cancelled for lack of candidates, or the seat's term ran to 2013. Unverified: an LA County 2011 cancelled-elections list would settle it.
- **Outcome evidence on both candidacies reads "Undated roster".** CEDA is a dated, sourced canvass compilation, so this label understates the evidence. Worth a look when the CEDA import is next reviewed.
