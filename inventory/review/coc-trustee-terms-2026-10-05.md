## Conventions

- **termDates**: Terms that began or ended at a regular election from 1995 are dated to December of the election year, the month the Education Code sets for the start of a term (footnoted); swearing-in dates, where a source gives one, are quoted in the footnotes. Earlier terms carry the precision their sources give.
- **edtfEither**: "1968/1969" style intervals mean one or the other, as the brief asks; "1973-05/1974-04" means some time in that span.
- **edtfBy**: "[..1992]" is EDTF level 2 for "1992 or earlier", used where a source shows only that the person was on the board by then.
- **selectionMethodBlank**: Empty where no source says how the term began (41 existing holdings use the same convention).
- **howEndedDefeat**: A term ended by defeat at the next election is "expired", as on the Hart holdings (e.g. #28801).
- **evidence**: CEDA and news reports: contemporary; the County's lists: certified; the district's board history: roster; the college histories, Leon Worden's captions and Carl Boyer's book: retrospective.
