## How sources are counted

- **Lifetime** means written while the person lived and the events were current, or an obituary at death (PROFILES.md, 'count the source set'). Fewer than three lifetime sources means a retrospective profile, said so.
- **Leon Worden's Hart roster** is primary for terms (PROFILES.md), but it is a later compilation from the district's lists, so it is never counted here as a lifetime source. Where it is the only source, the entry says so. Its 1993 error on Loberg and King is known (hart-pre1995 pass); this pass finds it silent on how Heidt's and Stroup's service ended, where the LA Times shows both defeated in 1991.
- Each source carries **ties**: yes (it names the trustee), probable (it names someone of the same name in the valley, very likely the trustee, but does not say so), or no (a lead only). Probable sources are not counted.
- Living or possibly living people (no death found): public life only. Phone-directory listings and family details were seen but are not quoted.

## Per person

### Jereann Bowman #28677: full profile

- **Names**: Jereann Bowman; Jereanne Bowman (Bowman High 1981 yearbook); Jerrean Bowman (Canyon High 1969 dedication program); Jeriann Bowman (Record-Press 1969); Mrs. Leland Bowman (Hart and Sierra Vista yearbooks 1965-66); Jeriann MacDougall (Bowman) (Westcott notes).
- **Life**: born 1921-03-13; died 1995-03-22, Murphys, Calif. (Dolan obituary 1995; Bowman High 1996 yearbook p. 6).
- **In the archive**: #28677, no body; personAliases empty. Holdings: #28761: 1964 to 1968, howEnded unknown, roster only; #28763: 1969 to 1969, resigned, roster only. Related: photograph #4801 (LW3007 reorganization plan; its index links 'Jereann Bowman 1963/69'); asset #1679 /gif/mugs/jereannbowmant.jpg (legacy mug thumbnail); article #12404 (Worden 1997, 'the old Bowman campus'). No record for Bowman High School. Her father Judge C.M. MacDougall has a legacy obituary page (jb_VNvalleynews082776).
- **Lifetime sources**: 8 (Dolan obituary 1995; SG7301 1973; Sierra Vista 1965; Hart 1966; Canyon 1969 program; Record-Press 1969; Valley News 1976 (family); Bowman 1981 yearbook. Also The Signal 1980 (garbled OCR) and LA Times 1989 (about the school).)
- **Recommendation**: full profile. Hub-like page exists (obituary_jereannbowman.htm links the Bowman yearbooks). Leads with the continuation school. Keep the 1920/1927 arrival and the 1963 and 1968 board questions as disagreements. A Bowman High School record (organization or place) does not exist and the profile would want one.

**In the valley, beyond the board**

- Came to Saugus in 1927 (Dolan 1995); came to Newhall in 1927 (Bowman 1996 yearbook); 'moving to Newhall in 1920' (Bowman 1981 yearbook). Disagreement kept.
- Daughter of Judge C.M. MacDougall (Dolan 1995; Valley News 1976).
- Drafted the bylaws of the Santa Clarita Valley Women's Auxiliary, the hospital volunteers, with Martha Barton and Gwen Gillespie, November 27, 1962 (Worden 2012, retrospective).
- President of the Soledad Township Coordinating Council, January 1969 (Record-Press, January 8, 1969, OCR).
- Driving force behind the district's continuation school, opened September 29, 1969 and named for her: Jereann Bowman High School (Dolan 1995; Bowman 1981 and 1996 yearbooks; LA Times 1989).
- 1973 SCV Woman of the Year, Newhall-Saugus-Valencia Chamber of Commerce (SG7301 caption; Dolan 1995; mwoty list); secretary-manager of the Chamber (Dolan 1995).
- Director of the Achievement Center, 1980 (The Signal, October 8, 1980, OCR).
- Lead only: ran MacDougall's Cafe at Solemint Junction ('driven by Jeriann MacDougall (Bowman)', Patricia Westcott Kelly's notes, 1990s). One recollection; not stated in a profile without another source.

**Board service**

- As held: #28761 1964-1968 (unknown) and #28763 1969 (resigned), from the roster.
- Roster: '1963' board prints four members without her; '1964-1967' board lists 'Jereann Bowman'; '1968' board does not list her; '1969' board lists 'Jereann Bowman (resigned)'.
- Dolan 1995 and the Bowman 1996 yearbook: 'served on the Hart board of trustees from 1963 to 1969'.
- Sierra Vista 1965 yearbook p. 4: 'Board Clerk Mrs. Leland Bowman' (the roster prints no offices before 1973).
- Hart 1966 yearbook p. 15: 'Mrs. Leland Bowman' among board members.
- Hart 1968 and Sierra Vista 1968 yearbooks list the board as Hoffner (clerk, the superintendent), Huntsinger, Agajanian, Ross, Schmidt, Duarte, without her. Agrees with the roster's 1968 board.
- Canyon High dedication program, October 15, 1969 (as reprinted 2018): board Huntsinger, Agajanian, Bowman, Duarte, Schmidt. So she was on the board, or still printed as on it, in October 1969; the resignation came later in the 1969 board year.
- Disagreement: Start: 1963 (obituary, 1996 yearbook) against the roster's first listing in 1964-1967 (its 1963 board has a vacant fifth seat, which fits a 1963 appointment). Continuity: the obituary's '1963 to 1969' against the roster and both 1968 yearbooks, which leave her off in 1968. Footnote both; state neither. Resignation date not found.

**In the valley, beyond the board**

- Managed the family hog ranch near Saugus (Haskell Canyon) for his father James T. Agajanian: 'Eli, manager of the hog ranch near Saugus' (Champion Spark Plug advertisement, 1953, transcribed on LW2866).
- Founded Blue Barrel Disposal Co. to haul food waste to the hog farm, and sold it in the 1960s (Worden 2017, citing Nazareth Chobanian, personal communication, 2017: retrospective).
- Founder and board chairman of Santa Clarita National Bank, opened in the mid-1960s; retired as chairman in 1975 (LA Times 1987; Canyon Call 1972; COC yearbook 1973; Dan Hon 1995).
- Director of the Upper Santa Clara Valley Water Agency, later Castaic Lake Water Agency, 1967-1974 (SCVHistory.com CLWA directors list; Worden 2017).
- Member of the committee that became the Henry Mayo Newhall Memorial Hospital board, 1970 (Worden 2012); honored as a hospital founder, 1985 (LA Times).
- General co-chairman of the Citizens' Committee to Complete College of the Canyons for the bond election of February 6, 1973 (The Canyon Call, October 31, 1972; COC 1973 yearbook).
- COC Foundation Silver Spur award, 1990 (SCVHistory.com list; Worden 2017).
- Newhall County Water District director: incumbent re-elected November 2, 1993, third of six for three seats with 1,592 votes (LA Times, November 4, 1993).
- Had served in the Army by the years after World War II, in an anecdote about a bank loan (Dan Hon 1995).
