## Which 42

## Method

1. Rebuilt the 42 from the audit JSON (`tier == 'B' and office >= 1`). Read each one's body and every candidacy and office holding with a temporary read-only PHP file under storage/, run by `ddev craft exec`, then deleted.
2. Fetched each board page once with curl (Safari user agent). Saved copies with sha256 are in the session scratchpad (`recount/manifest.txt`): saugususd.org/governing-board; hartdistrict.org/governing-board; newhallschooldistrict.com/governing-board-members; sssd.k12.ca.us/members; castaicusd.com board and Pearson profile; yourscvwater.com/governance/board-directors; santaclarita.gov/city-council; canyons.edu board; plus three SCV News articles (Bryce, Strickland, Colley).
3. One or two standard web searches per person.
4. Ran `grep -rliE --include="*.htm*"` for each full name, and common short forms, over /Volumes/Reggie/SCVHistory/scvhistory.com.
5. Classes: (a) the body's own site has a biography; (b) independent news, an obituary or an official release gives enough for three to five sentences; (c) only letters, endorsements or candidate roundups; (d) nothing beyond one line. Wikipedia and Ballotpedia were used only as finding aids.

Not done: Wayback copies of past board pages (likely to turn up biographies for the (d) former trustees of Hart and Castaic from about 2000 to 2010), and the Signal's own archive.
