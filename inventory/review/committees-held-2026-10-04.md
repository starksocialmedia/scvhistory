## What a home for these would need

This is my reading, for Nathan to decide; nothing below is built.

- **The model already fits.**
  - A seat on a body that is itself an office (the Planning Commission, an elected county committee)
    is an office holding on that body.
  - A delegate or member seat (SCAG, the Measure V committee, a joint powers authority) is an
    affiliation of kind "member", with the title as printed ("Delegate", "Alternate", "Member") and
    the years.
- **Which bodies get records.** Each body needs an organization record only where it recurs in the
  valley's history (the full-records rule). The Measure V committee, the City Formation Committee,
  the Planning Commission, SCAG's district seat and the North County Transportation Coalition
  plausibly do. The council's internal standing committees probably do not, and could be listed
  from the City's yearly sheet without records.
- **Where they show.**
  - On a body's page: a Committees tab, or a section further down.
  - On a person's page: the seats under Public life, after the offices.

## Decisions for Nathan

1. Which of these bodies get their own records. My recommendation is the five recurring ones named
   above, to start.
2. Whether the City's yearly appointments sheet is recorded each year, as affiliations dated to that
   year. That would give every council member's regional seats with years, from a primary source.
3. Where committees sit on the body hub: a tab of their own, or inside "In the archive".
