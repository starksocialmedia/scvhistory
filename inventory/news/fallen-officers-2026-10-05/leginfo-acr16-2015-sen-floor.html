           SUBJECT:   Los Angeles County Sheriffs Deputy Shayne Daniel  
                     York Memorial Highway


          SOURCE:    Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department

          DIGEST:   This resolution designates a portion of Interstate 5  
          in Los Angeles County as the Los Angeles County Sheriff's Deputy  
          Shayne Daniel York Memorial Highway.

          ANALYSIS:    This resolution designates a portion of Interstate  
          5 between Newhall Ranch Road and Hasley Canyon Road in Los  
          Angeles County as the Los Angeles County Sheriff's Deputy Shayne  
          Daniel York Memorial Highway.  This resolution further requests  
          that the Department of Transportation erect appropriate signs  
          upon receiving donations from non-state sources to cover the  
          costs.  
          
          Comments
          
          Purpose.  The author introduced this resolution to honor the

          Fullerton Police Departments later apprehended the two
