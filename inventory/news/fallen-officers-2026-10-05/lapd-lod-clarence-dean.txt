Officers Killed in the Line of Duty
Officers Killed in the Line of Duty
Clarence Dean
Rank: Police Officer
Serial Number: 13994
Division: Valley Traffic
Location: 5 Fwy and 14 Fwy interchange
Date Killed: Monday, January 17, 1994
Cause of Death: Motorcycle Traffic Accident during Earthquake 
Rememberance
Officer Dean was killed when his motorcycle drove off of a section of freeway which had just collapsed due to the Northridge earthquake.
Officer Dean had been with the Department for 26 years and is survived by his son and daughter.
Heroism. Valor. In memoriam.
Every day, the members of the LAPD put their lives on the line. We remember and honor the officers who have gone above and beyond the call of duty who have lost their lives while serving the people of the City of Los Angeles.
