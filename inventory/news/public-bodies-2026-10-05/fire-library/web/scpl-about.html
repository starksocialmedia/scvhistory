About the Santa Clarita Public Library

The mission of the Santa Clarita Public Library is to bring people, information and ideas together to educate, inspire and enrich the quality of life in our diverse community.

The mission of the Santa Clarita Public Library is to bring people, information, and ideas together to educate, inspire and enrich the quality of life in our diverse community.

On July 1, 2011, the City of Santa Clarita officially opened its doors to the Santa Clarita Public Library.

Our library is comprised of three branches: Canyon Country Jo Anne Darcy, Old Town Newhall and Valencia.  

We hope that you enjoy your Santa Clarita Public Libraries and will let us know if you have any thoughts or suggestions about improving your library experience.

Santa Clarita Public Library Statistics

The Santa Clarita Public Library proudly served over
440,000 patrons during the 2024-2025 fiscal year,
marking a 9 percent increase from the previous fiscal
year. Across our three branches, we offered over 1,400
programs aiming to strengthen community
connections and provide meaningful ways to engage.
With a continued focus on education, inspiration and
enrichment, we look forward to building on this success
and serving the community through the 2025-2026
fiscal year.
