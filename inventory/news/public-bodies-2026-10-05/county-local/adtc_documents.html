Nominating Period is Now Open
Sep 15th, 2026
This just in from the Election Committee: Nominating period is now open 09/15/2026; applications are available online via ADTC Website at www.adtowncouncil.com, local social media outlets and via email from ADTC and the ADTC Election Committee. They will also be placed at the Agua Dulce Hardware Store, 33314 Agua Dulce Canyon Rd. and at several common areas in town. Please remember to keep your voter registration current. There are currently four (4) seats open for a 2-year term. Incumbents are Kathryn Segura, Cliff Grimes, and Mary Johnson. Those terms expire in December 2026. Two incumbents have declared their intent to apply for re-election, Kathryn Segura and Cliff Grimes. read more

August Meeting is CANCELLED

The regular August Meeting of the Agua Dulce Town Council is cancelled for Summer Break. The next regular meeting will be September 9, 2026. That meeting will be in-person/hybrid at the Agua Dulce Women's Club, 33201 Agua Dulce Canyon Rd, Agua Dulce. read more

      • The Brown Act: Open Meetings for Local Legislative Bodies, pdf   Provides a brief overview of the main provisions of the Ralph M. Brown Act, which govern open meetings for local legislative bodies. This pamphlet is intended to assist local legislative bodies in complying with California's open meeting laws and to assist those who monitor the performance of legislative bodies.(NOTE: The Brown Act has been modified and elements changed through litigiation since it was written in 2003. However, it is helpful to read in order to better understand the broad concepts of the act.)
