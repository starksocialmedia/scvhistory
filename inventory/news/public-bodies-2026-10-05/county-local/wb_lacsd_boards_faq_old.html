Are the meetings open to the public?

Yes. All board and committee meetings are open to the public, in accordance to the Ralph M. Brown Act.

Are your agendas online?
Click here to view or print current Boards Agendas.
Can the public be recognized and speak at the meetings?

Yes, the public can be recognized and speak at the meetings.  Sign-up forms are available and completed forms are submitted to the Secretary to the Boards of Directors.

When, how often, and where do the Directors meet?

District No. 2, the administrative district, meets at 1:30 p.m. on the 2nd and 4th Wednesdays of each month at the Joint Administration Office (JAO) in Whittier. 

Seventeen districts meet once per month, either at 1:30 p.m. on the 2nd or 4th Wednesdays at JAO or at 1:30 p.m. on the 3rd Wednesday at Torrance City Hall. 

Six districts meet quarterly either at 1:30 p.m. on the 2nd or 4th Wednesday at JAO or 9:30 a.m. on the 2nd Tuesday at the Kenneth Hahn Hall of Administration.
