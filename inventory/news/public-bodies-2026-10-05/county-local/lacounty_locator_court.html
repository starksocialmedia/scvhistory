Los Angeles County Superior Court - North Valley District - Santa Clarita

This state trial court is an L A County Superior Court with jurisdiction over cases that take place in the city of Santa Clarita and unincorporated areas inlcuding Canyon Country, Castaic, Newhall, Saugus, Stevenson Ranch, and Val Verde.

Address: 23747 W. Valencia Blvd. Santa Clarita CA 91355
Website: https://www.lacourt.org/
Hours:
  • Monday through Friday, 8:30am to 4:30pm. Telephone hours are Monday through Friday, 8:30am to 10:30am and 1:30pm to 3:30pm.
Phones:
  • Criminal Court Service/Intake (661) 253-7384, Administrative (661) 253-7301, Community Service Service/Intake (661) 253-7247, Probation Service/Intake (661) 253-7271, Automated Traffic Line Service/Intake (213) 742-6648, Civil Court Service/Intake (661)
