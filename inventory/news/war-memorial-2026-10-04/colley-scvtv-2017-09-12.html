SCV NewsBreak | September 12, 2017: Suicide Victim ID, Knight Letter, more

Uploaded 09/12/2017

L.A. County coroners have identified a man who killed himself last week at Oak Tree Gun Club.

U.S. Rep. Steve Knight termed President Donald Trump’s pardon of former Maricopa County Sheriff Joe Arpaio “improper” in a letter to a constituent Monday.

Dozens attended the Latino Business Alliance Hispanic Heritage Gala on Saturday at the Canyon Theatre Guild.

Are you wondering how to prevent your teen from drug abuse? The city of Santa Clarita is hosting a parent resource symposium Wednesday evening with local experts.

Plus, weather.
