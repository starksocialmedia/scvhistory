 Santa Clarita Valley Sheriff’s Station Celebrates 100 Years of Service 
 Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department | Wednesday, Aug 26, 2026

 The Santa Clarita Valley Sheriff’s Station celebrated its 100th anniversary on Wednesday, Aug. 26, marking a century of dedicated service, partnership and commitment to the communities of the Santa Clarita Valley. The celebration was held at the Santa Clarita Sheriff’s Station, 26201 Golden Valley Road, Santa Clarita, CA 91350.
 For 100 years, the men and women who have served at the Santa Clarita Valley Sheriff’s Station have stood alongside residents, businesses, community leaders, and partner agencies to protect lives, preserve public safety and build lasting relationships throughout the SCV.
 Over the past century, the Santa Clarita Valley has experienced extraordinary growth and change. Through it all, the Sheriff’s Station has evolved alongside the community, adapting to new challenges while maintaining the fundamental principles of service, integrity, professionalism and compassion.
 “Our responsibility is to honor the legacy of those who came before us by continuing to serve with integrity, compassion, professionalism and respect,” Los Angeles County Sheriff Robert Luna said. “That was our mission 100 years ago, and it remains our mission today. And it will continue to be our mission for generations to come.”
 The centennial anniversary is an opportunity to recognize the generations of deputies, professional staff, volunteers, community partners and residents who have contributed to the station’s history.
 It is also a moment to recognize the families of those who have served. Law enforcement is a commitment that extends beyond the badge, and the support of families has played an important role in allowing deputies and professional staff to serve their community.
 “This is more than a celebration of 100 years, it is a celebration of the men and women who have worn the badge, the families who have supported them, and the community that has entrusted us with the responsibility of keeping them safe,” said Captain Brandon Barclay, Santa Clarita Valley Sheriff’s Station. “We are incredibly proud of our history, but our greatest responsibility is the future. As we celebrate this milestone, we remain committed to providing the highest level of service to the people of the Santa Clarita Valley.”
 The Santa Clarita Valley Sheriff’s Station extends its sincere appreciation to the residents of the Valley and to all community, civic, business, nonprofit, and public-safety partners who have helped make the Santa Clarita Valley a remarkable place to live, work and raise a family.
 One hundred years of service. One community. One shared commitment to a safer Santa Clarita Valley.
