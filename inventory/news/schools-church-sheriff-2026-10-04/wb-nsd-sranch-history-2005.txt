Letter from The Principal 
 Welcome! 
 Thank you for visiting our Stevenson Ranch School Website. 
 Stevenson Ranch
Elementary School is located in Stevenson Ranch, California, a suburban area thirty miles
north of Los Angeles, serving the northwestern portion of the Newhall School
District. The school was originally built in 1995 to accommodate 930 students in kindergarten
through sixth grade, with three Special Day Classes. An
interim campus located .8 mile from the central campus was added in August 1999 to
accommodate growth. The central campus
presently houses 1,090 students with the north campus housing 414 students. S tevenson Ranch Elementary School, along with five
other schools in the Newhall School District, operates on a year round
multi-track calendar in which approximately 75% of the enrolled student
population is on campus at any one time. North
campus follows the Red Track calendar. 
 Surrounded by hills, new housing developments, and established residential areas, our
school has a strong neighborhood identity. The
community supports the traditional values of honesty, responsibility, patriotism, and
pride in work. Stevenson Ranch School teaches
these same values and works closely with the local community in pursuing high
expectations, academic excellence, and strong community involvement. We are proud to be named a 2001
National Blue Ribbon School and 1998 California
Distinguished School. 
 Our schools motto First in Excellence, First in Pride 
is shared by students, staff, and the local community. 
 It plays a fundamental role in the instructional program. 
 Parents, community members, and staff work closely together to exemplify
for our children that school is an important place and that our school is a
learning community for all. It is
in this caring environment that a rich, thinking curriculum unfolds each day,
and children, parents, and staff learn together at Stevenson Ranch School. 
 We hope you enjoy your tour of our website and find a
school committed to excellence! 
 Sincerely, 
 Diane von Buelow 
 Principal
