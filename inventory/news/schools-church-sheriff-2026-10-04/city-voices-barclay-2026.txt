 Santa Clarita Voices Podcast Welcomes Santa Clarita Valley Sheriff’s Station Captain Brandon Barclay
 Episode Explores E-Bike Enforcement, Red Light Runners and the People Behind Local Law Enforcement 
 The City of Santa Clarita is excited to announce the release of a new episode of Santa Clarita Voices , the City’s official podcast, featuring Santa Clarita Valley Sheriff’s Station Captain Brandon Barclay. Hosted by Santa Clarita City Manager, Ken Striplin, this episode focuses on the growing concern surrounding unsafe e-bike and e-motorcycle usage, the dangers posed by red light runners and more.
 In addition to discussing current enforcement priorities, the conversation also highlights the strong morale and sense of teamwork within the Santa Clarita Valley Sheriff’s Station. Santa Clarita Voices viewers and listeners will also get a more personal look at Captain Barclay, as he shares stories about his background, leadership philosophy and life outside of the Santa Clarita Valley Sheriff’s Station.
 Santa Clarita Voices features conversations with City leaders, staff and community members, offering an inside look at the projects, places and stories that shape Santa Clarita. Episodes are available with video on Spotify and the City’s YouTube channel ( YouTube.com/@SantaClaritaGov ), as well as audio on Apple Podcasts. Be sure to subscribe so you can be notified when a new episode is released!
