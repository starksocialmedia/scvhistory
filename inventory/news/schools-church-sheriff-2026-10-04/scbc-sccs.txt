 Santa clarita christian school
 Founded in September 1982, Santa Clarita Christian School was founded as a ministry of SCBC serving grades K-12.
Under the leadership of SCBC, our mission is to partner with Christian parents of like faith to challenge students’ minds and train their hearts for God through a distinctively biblical education and produce students who are prepared to make a difference in the world for God.
Committed to both academic excellence and faithful discipleship, SCCS is overseen by the leadership of our church, including one of our elders, Dr. Mark Wilson. We don’t just educate our students to pass a test or course, we prepare them to live a life that is Christ-centered, academically sound and expressed to its fullest so it might positively impact the world.
