Leading with Vision and Purpose 
All Students Learning at High Levels
Dear Sulphur Springs Union School District Community,
As we begin a new school year, our schools are filled with the excitement of new beginnings—new classrooms, new friendships, new opportunities, and new possibilities for learning and growth.
At the center of all that we do is one student at a time.
Our work as educators is grounded in a simple but powerful belief: every student can learn at high levels. Our responsibility is to create the conditions that make that possible for every child, every day.
Learning at high levels means more than achieving academic success. It means knowing our students well, understanding what they need to grow, and providing engaging, rigorous instruction that challenges them to think deeply, ask questions, solve problems, and persevere when learning is difficult. It means ensuring that every student has access to the support and opportunities necessary to reach their full potential.
We are committed to educating the whole child. Students need to feel safe, connected, valued, and confident in their ability to learn. They need strong relationships with caring adults, opportunities to develop independence and responsibility, and the skills to navigate challenges and build meaningful relationships with others. When students feel that they belong and know that adults believe in them, they are better positioned to engage deeply in their learning.
Our commitment extends beyond academic achievement. We want our students to leave our schools as confident learners, compassionate individuals, critical thinkers, effective communicators, and responsible members of their communities.
This important work cannot be accomplished by educators alone. It takes a partnership among our teachers, support staff, administrators, families, and community. Together, we have the opportunity to create schools where every student is known, every voice matters, and every child is encouraged to discover what they are capable of achieving.
All students learning at high levels.
I look forward to an incredible school year filled with learning, growth, and possibility.
With appreciation,
Dr. Catherine Kawaguchi
Superintendent of Schools
