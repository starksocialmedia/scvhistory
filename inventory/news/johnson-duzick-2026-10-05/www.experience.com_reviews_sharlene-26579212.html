About
Sharlene has been in the real estate industry for the past 8 years and a long time resident of the Santa Clarita Valley. Her passion lies with helping support the residents and shape her local community. She has been involved with non-profit since her teen years supporting organizations such as Single Mothers Outreach, the Chamber of Commerce, Henry Mayo Hospital and YMCA. Sharlene also has served on William S Hart School District Bond Measure SA Oversight Committee, sits on several local boards and committees such as: the WiSH Education Foundation, College of the Canyons Foundation Board, JCI Santa Clarita and the Valley Industrial Association's (VIA) education team. Locally she champions young leadership development in order to support the communities education and commerce infrastructure. She believes that our job as an agent is to be involved in the community we represent as well as educate, support and negotiate for clients. You are not just another sale, you become part of our family and that is why personal touch matters in this industry to her. A quote she lives by is "the meaning of life is finding your gift; the purpose of life is giving it away".Santa Clarita is also their home, so giving back and being involved is important. Sharlene serves on The College of the Canyons Foundation, California Junior Chamber, Wish Education Foundation, Valley Industrial Association, 38th Assembly District and more. Jessica serves on Castaic Area Town Council, Chiquita Canyon Landfill Community Advisory Committee, SCV Junior Chamber International, and SCV Sunrise Rotary.
