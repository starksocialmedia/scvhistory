About Sharlene

Sharlene Johnson has a gift for making people feel seen—and for helping them recognize what they’re capable of. She has spent her life putting that gift to work in service of others. In real estate, that means listening closely, advocating fiercely, and making sure every client knows how much their move matters. As a Santa Clarita Valley Realtor with RE/MAX Gateway and Team Lead of The Sharlene Johnson Team, Sharlene begins with the person behind the move. She takes the time to understand the full story before building a plan around it, then helps her clients make sense of what matters, what comes next, and which questions to ask so they can move forward with clarity and confidence. Sharlene began her real estate career alongside her dad in 2014. From him, she gained a deep respect for research, regulation, and the details that protect people. Sharlene brought compassion, intuition, and a genuine curiosity about the people behind each decision. Together, those strengths shaped the way she serves today: with personal care, careful preparation, and steady follow-through. Raised in Agua Dulce and rooted in Canyon Country, Sharlene’s local knowledge is lived, not memorized. It comes from decades of family life, community service, and showing up for the people and organizations that make the Santa Clarita Valley feel like home. Her roots show up in how she serves, not just where she works. Under Sharlene’s leadership, The Sharlene Johnson Team has built a strong record of client transactions and reached nearly $20 million in annual sales volume—proof that meaningful relationships and high-execution real estate can go hand in hand. Whether she’s guiding a first-time buyer, helping a family sell a long-held home, or supporting a past client through a new chapter, Sharlene listens like a neighbor and goes to bat like an advocate. She believes every stage deserves respect, every move is personal, and the property matters—but the person matters more.
