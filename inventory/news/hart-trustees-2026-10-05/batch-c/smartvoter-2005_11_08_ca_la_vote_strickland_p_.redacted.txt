 Biographical Highlights 
Occupation: Incumbent
Producer of Animated Children's Programming
President, Santa Clarita Arts Advisory Committee
Co-chair, Hart District ad hoc Committee on Diversity
22-year Santa Clarita resident with one child still attending school in the District
Former Board Member of Sulphur Springs Union Elementary School District
Hart District representative to the Los Angeles County Trustees Association 
 Top Priorities if Elected 
Helping achieve student success.
Helping provide a quality education in a safe environment for all students.
Committed to the maintenance of our award-winng public school system
