Los Angeles County, CA 
 November 6, 2007 Election 
 Full Biography for Gloria Mercado-Fortine 
 Candidate for
Governing Board Member; William S. Hart Union High School District 
 This information is provided by the candidate 
Experience
Current Governing Board Member, William S. Hart Union High School District
District Administrator, Los Angeles Unified School District
Former teacher, counselor and administrator at middle, junior and high school levels
Educational consultant specializing in school reform, charter schools, federal and state compliance audits
Former adjunct professor for California State University, Los Angeles; Mission College and College of the Canyons
Lifelong resident of Santa Clarita Valley and graduate of William S. Hart Union High School District
Has served as secondary principal in the third largest school in the United States.
Has served as teacher, counselor and administrator in some of the toughest and most socio-economic, ethnic and culturally diverse schools in the nation.
Education
Master of science degrees in school management and administration from Pepperdine University
Bachelor of arts degree from California State University, Northridge
Graduate of William S. Hart High School
Masters in governance, California School Boards Association
Trainer in cultural diversity
Past member, Advisory Committee, Masters Degree Educational Administration Program, Pepperdine University
Elected Offices
Currently serving second four-year term as a publicly elected governing board member of the William S. Hart Union High School District
Served as a publicly elected member of the governing board of the Castaic Union School District
Elected Member, Castaic Town Council
Volunteer Activities
Executive Board of the City of Santa Clarita/Sheriff's Anti-Gang Task Force
Board of Directors of the Santa Clarita Valley Boys & Girls Club
Past president and member of Zonta Club of Santa Clarita Valley, an international women's organization working to advance the status of women worldwide
Executive Board of the Child and Family Center
Board of Directors of the Prevention Policy Board of the Santa Clarita Valley, Office of Criminal Justice and Planning
Past Board of Directors, Youth Project
Co-Chair, Ad-hoc Committee on Diversity, Wm. S. Hart School District
Volunteer, Michael Hoefflin Foundation for Children's Cancer
Executive Board, Fourth of July Parade and Parade Announcer
Volunteer, SCV Senior Center Wine Classic
Volunteer, Boys and Girls Club Annual Auction
Founding President, Santa Clarita Valley Unit, American Cancer Society
Volunteer, Healthy Woman's Day Screening, Samuel Dixon Clinic, Zonta Club
Chair/ Member, Healthy Kids Club, Val Verde
Youth Scholarship Committee, Association of Mexican American Administrators
Former Member, Michael Hoefflin Foundation Advisory Board
Former Member, Board of Directors, Girl Scouts of America, Joshua Tree Council
Past Steering Committee Member, Measure V and S, Hart District School Bond
Founding Member, Castaic Town Council
Past Member, Board of Directors, Aid to Victims of Domestic Violence
Honors/Recognition
Received first-annual 2004 Amistad Award, Individual Community Leader of the Year, Santa Clarita Valley Chamber of Commerce, Hispanic Business Committee
Nominated for 2005 Santa Clarita Valley Woman of the Year.
Nominated for Zonta Woman of the Year
Girl Scouts, awarded lifetime membership
Recognized by federal, state and local legislators for outstanding volunteerism
Educator of the Year, Hollywood Coordinating Council
Nominated Educator of the Year, Los Angeles Unified School District
Recipient, Ford Fellowship in Education
Recognized by LAUSD Board of Education and spotlighted in a documentary by KLCS-TV for raising test scores and improving the school and community environment in a gang-controlled area
