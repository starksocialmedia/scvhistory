BOARD  OF  TRUSTEES






























































                             Standing  left  to  righl:  Mr.  T.  M.  Frew;  Mr.  C.  l.  Dillenbeck;  Mr.  S.  S.  Donaldson.
                             Seated:  Mrs.  Mildred  0.  Gilmour;  Mrs.  Mary  P.  Bonelli.

                       Democracy  is  based  upon  a  belief  in  the  inestimable  worth  of  the  individual.
                   Followers  of  this  doctrine  conceive  the  welfare  of  the  people,  of  individual  persons,
                   as  the  true  end  of all  social  effort.  They  believe  in  their  own  creative  abilities  and
                   are  encouraged  to  dream  about  the  good  life  which  they  would  build  for  themselves
                   and  their  children.  They  lay  plans  for  the  society  of  tomorrow.  We  are  grateful  to
                   the  men  and  women  of  vision  who  comprise  our  Board.  They  provide  ways  of  getting
                   things  done;  through  them  the  ideals  of  a  community  become  realities.
