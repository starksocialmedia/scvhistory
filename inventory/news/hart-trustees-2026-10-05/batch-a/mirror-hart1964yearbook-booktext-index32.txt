Administration



         Board  of  Trustees

















































                 Ringing the bell symbolic of freedom in education are the members of Hart's  Board of

              Trustees.  From left to right they  are Mr.  Curtis R.  Huntsinger,  board president; Dr.

              William D.  Ross,  clerk;  Mrs.  Edith S.  Palmer,  member;  Mr.  Earl Smidt,  member;  Mrs.

              Jereann Bowman,  alternate clerk;  and Mr.  James R.  Hoffner,  secretary.

                 The members  of the  Board are firm believers in the importance of education and are

              dedicated school  servants.  They provide basic school policies and procedures.

                 Board members,  utilizing their years of experience,  devote long hours of their time to

              school problems.
