Mm-mm — Is That Good!
Newhall-Saugus Republican Women's Club, Federated, Cookbook.
Bobbie Trueblood and Joyce Bonelli, Editors | Illustrations by Bobbie Trueblood.
Newhall, California | n.d. (1964).

Abstract.

Mm-mm — is that good! is presented for your pleasure and enjoyment by the Newhall-Saugus Republican Women's Club. However, adding a little spice to the cook book are some good democrat cooks, too!

These recipes, some old, some new, some original, some from other favorite cook books, some from Great-Aunt Hessie, some from modern mixes, all blend together to give you well-tried recipes from the kitchens of some excellent cooks in our Valley.

Bobbie Trueblood, Joyce Bonelli
