Document Description
On October 15, 2017, Senate Bill 634 was signed into law by Governor Brown, thereby creating the Santa Clarita Valley Water Agency ("SCVWA"). SB 634 requires that on or before January 31, 2018, the SCVWA shall submit an applicaiton for conditions addressing the creation of the SCVWA, including among other items a plan for providing services, to the Local Agency Formation Commission ("LAFCO") for the County of Los Angeles (the "Application for Conditions"). On January 29, 2018m the Board of Directors of SCVWA approved the submittal of the Application for Conditions and plan for services in accordance wih SB 634.
