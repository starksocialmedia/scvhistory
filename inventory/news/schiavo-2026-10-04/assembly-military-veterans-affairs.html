Welcome to the Committee on Military and Veterans Affairs

Assembly Member Pilar Schiavo Assembly District 40 Chair of the Military and Veterans Affairs Committee
Assembly Member Jeff Gonzalez Assembly District 36 Vice Chair of the Military and Veterans Affairs Committee
For information on how to submit a position letter please see the  
Advocacy Quick Reference Guide

Committee Jurisdiction: Primary areas of interest are the California Department of Veterans Affairs (CalVet) and its programs, including the Farm and Home Loan Program, the VHHP, the CVSO Subvention Program and the Veterans Homes; and the California Military Department and its units, operations and programs, including the National Guard, the State Guard, CMD youth programs and the Cyber Network Defense team.

The Committee on Military and Veterans Affairs is located in the Legislative Office Building, 1020 N Street, Room 389 and phone number is (916) 319-3550.

We encourage the public to provide written testimony before the hearing by visiting https://calegislation.lc.ca.gov/Advocates/. Please note that any written testimony submitted to the committee is considered public comment and may be read into the record or reprinted.

Procedures put in place to accommodate the pandemic restrictions have been reverted back to pre-pandemic practices. Teleconference testimony during committee hearings will no longer be available.
