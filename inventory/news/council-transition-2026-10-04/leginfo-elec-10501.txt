UNIFORM DISTRICT ELECTION LAW [10500 - 10556]  ( Part 4 enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2. )  10501.  It is the purpose of this part to provide a procedure for the election of elective officers of districts. These elections shall be called and conducted and the results canvassed, returned, and declared pursuant to this part.
(Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
