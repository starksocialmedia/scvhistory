ARTICLE 2. Order and Call of Elections [5320 - 5329]  ( Article 2 enacted by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1010. )
  5328.  If pursuant to Section 5326 a district election is not held, the qualified person or persons nominated shall be seated at the organizational meeting of the board, or if no person has been nominated or if an insufficient number is nominated, the governing board shall appoint a qualified person or persons, as the case may be, at a meeting prior to the day fixed for the election, and such appointee or appointees shall be seated at the organizational meeting of the board as if elected at a district election.
(Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 22.)
