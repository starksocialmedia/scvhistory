                                                         CITY OF SANTA CLARITA

                                                                    MEMORANDUM


TO:                       Interested Parties

FROM:                     Mary Cusick, City Clerk

DATE:                     August 10, 2024

SUBJECT:                  LIST OF QUALIFIED CANDIDATES


The following individuals, presented in no particular order, are qualified candidates for the
City’s General Municipal Election, November 5, 2024.

For the purpose of electing one Councilmember to District 1:
    Tim Burkhart
    Bryce Jepsen
    Patsy Ayala

For the purpose of electing one Councilmember to District 3:
    Jason Gibbs

Addresses and phone numbers for qualified candidates will not be published on the City’s
election webpage. Government Code Section 6254.21 restricts the posting of an elected or
appointed official's home address or telephone number. In certain cases, such posting
could result in a misdemeanor violation.

You may request a copy of the Certified List of Qualified Candidates, with contact information
included, by calling the City Clerk’s Office at (661) 255-4391.
