Members

Board of Trustees
The Sulphur Springs Union School District Governing Board held its Annual Organizational Meeting on December 18th. The Governing Board officers for 2025-2026 were elected, and they are as follows:
 
Board President: Shelley Weinstein
Clerk: Ken Chase
Board Member: Denis DeFigueiredo
Board Member: Lori MacDonald
Board Member: Paola Jellings
 

Expand AllCollapse All
	•	Shelley Weinstein   Shelley Weinstein   Board President  Trustee Area No. 1  Mrs. Weinstein has served on the Board of Trustees since 2003.  She is a past president of the SCV School Trustees Association and served for five years as a Director on the Executive Board of the LA County School Trustees Association.  Before being on the school board, Mrs. Weinstein served on various district committees and was an active PTA board member and a community volunteer. She was honored to receive the PTA Honorary Service and Golden Oak awards, the ACSA Golden Apple award, and being named a “Champion of Children” by the Cystic Fibrosis Foundation.  Mrs. Weinstein was a Vice President in the banking industry for 20 years and worked at College of the Canyons for over 10 years. Mrs. Weinstein and her husband, Steve, have three adult children, all of whom attended Sulphur Springs Community School. 
	•	Ken Chase    Ken Chase   Clerk  Trustee Area No. 4  Mr. Chase is a Certified Management Accountant. He is the Corporate Controller for Park Group in Pasadena. Mr. Chase has been active with the Sulphur Springs School District since 2004, serving on school site councils and various committees. He was chairperson of the Measure CK Bond Oversight Committee. He and his wife, Debby, have lived in Newhall since 1992. Their two daughters attended Valley View and Golden Oak Community schools. In support of his daughter, who has Type 1 diabetes, his family is very active in volunteering and supporting the JDRF (Juvenile Diabetes Research Foundation). Mr. Chase is honored to have been named the Los Angeles Chapter’s volunteer of the year. He was elected to the Sulphur Springs Union School Board of Trustees in 2013.    
	•	Denis DeFigueiredo   Denis DeFigueiredo  Board Member  Trustee Area No. 2   Mr. DeFigueiredo and his family have been residents of Canyon Country since 1991. Mr. DeFigueiredo is an Information Technology professional. He and his wife Sharon have three children, all of whom attended Mitchell Community School. Mr. DeFigueiredo has served on School Site Councils at Mitchell Elementary and Sierra Vista Junior High and on district committees in the Hart District. His involvement in the community and schools, including youth sports, music, and Scouting, has been recognized by the PTA with an Honorary Service Award and by the Boy Scouts of America with a District Award of Merit.  "Education is a series of partnerships between parents, students, teachers, and administrators. The goal of those partnerships must be to provide an educational environment where each child realizes his or her highest potential by consistently recognizing and doing what's best for kids." 
	•	Lori MacDonald   Lori MacDonald    Board Member  Trustee Area No. 5  Mrs. MacDonald and her husband have been happily married since 2000 and have been Canyon Country residents since 2002. They have three children who graduated from the SSUSD: Leona Cox Community and Golden Oak Community schools. They moved into the Hart District and graduated from Sierra Vista and La Mesa Junior High schools, then Canyon and Golden Valley High Schools. (Their youngest will graduate in 2026 from GVHS.)  Mrs. MacDonald began teaching for LAUSD in 1999 as a middle school Physical Education teacher. She has also taught Health, Science, and a technology elective. Education is part of her family history: her mother, father, several aunts, and mother-in-law were teachers. In 2012, Mrs. MacDonald earned her Master's in Administrative Educational Services from CSU, Northridge. She has served as a member of several School Site Councils and is active in the PTA. Mrs. MacDonald assisted in passing Measure CK in 2012 to get funding for needed repairs, construction, and technology updates in the Sulphur Springs schools. She is active locally in youth sports.  Mrs. MacDonald has been honored to serve on the Sulphur Springs Union School Board since 2012. 
	•	Paola Jellings   Paola Jellings   Board Member  Trustee Area No. 3  Mrs. Jellings has been a Sulphur Springs Union School Board member since June 2020. She is a first-generation Latina American and has been a Canyon Country resident for over 30 years. She and her husband, Kyle, have six children. Mrs. Jellings stays home with three of them. There are three current students within the district.  Before being able to stay home with her children full-time, Mrs. Jellings worked for over 10 years at various school sites and districts within Santa Clarita in a myriad of capacities. Her academic background includes a bachelor’s degree in Sociology from Brigham Young University and a master’s degree in School Counseling from the University of La Verne.  Although this is her first experience serving on a Board in the Santa Clarita Valley, Mrs. Jellings is excited and humbled by the opportunity to once again work alongside staff and students in a new capacity.
