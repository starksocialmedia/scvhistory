Laura Arrowsmith was a member of the Saugus Union School District school board in California, representing Trustee Area 2. She assumed office in 2018. She left office on December 9, 2022.
Arrowsmith ran for re-election to the Saugus Union School District school board to represent Trustee Area 2 in California. She lost in the general election on November 8, 2022.
