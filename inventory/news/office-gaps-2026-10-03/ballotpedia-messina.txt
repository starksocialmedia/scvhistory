Joe Messina is a member of the William S. Hart Union High School District Board of Trustees in California, representing Trustee Area 5. He assumed office in 2009. His current term ends on December 11, 2026.
Messina is running for re-election to the William S. Hart Union High School District Board of Trustees to represent Trustee Area 5 in California. He is on the ballot in the general election on November 3, 2026.[source]

Joe Messina ran against two other candidates, including fellow incumbents Paul B. Strickland and Bob Jensen for three seats in the general election on November 5, 2013. The election has been canceled and will not appear on the official ballot.[1]
