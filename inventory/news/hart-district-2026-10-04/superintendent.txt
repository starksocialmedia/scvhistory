 One Hart: Every Student - Opportunity Ready 
 Welcome to the William S. Hart Union High School District!   
 As a eighteen-year Santa Clarita resident and fourteen-year member of the Hart District Team, it is an honor to serve as Superintendent after serving as both Assistant Superintendent Human Resources and Deputy Superintendent, Educational Services in the District. I look forward to continuing the work of supporting every student through the implementation of our Strategic Plan.   
 The Hart District’s Strategic Plan was developed in 2022 to ensure that every student receives a high quality educational experience and can pursue any career path upon graduation - One Hart: Every Student Opportunity Ready .  The Plan is built upon the Core Values of Community , Caring , Creativity and Courage with Five Focus Areas: 
 Student Learning and Instruction 
 Safety and Wellness 
 Access and Equity 
 Financial and Human Resources 
 Communication and Collaboration 
 For decades, Hart District Schools have been recognized as exemplary because of our staff, programs, facilities, and community that reflect the tenets of our Strategic Plan.  We strive to continue with and improve these educational experiences.  Some of our honors and awards include: 
 Recognized as a California Exemplary District 
 Thirteen Schools Designated as California Distinguished Schools 
 Bowman High School Named a Model Continuation High School (every year since 1999) 
 Eight High Schools Nationally Ranked Among Best Public High Schools in the Nation ( U.S. News and World Report ) 
 Six High Schools Among the Top 10% for Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEAM - Newsweek ) 
 Seven Los Angeles County Teachers of the Year in the last decade 
 Two California State Teachers of the Year and a State Teacher of the Year finalist in the last decade 
 96.8% Graduation Rate 
 90% Students Continue to Post-Secondary Education & Training 
 Finally, we value our community and we invite feedback and collaboration.  Please contact us with any questions, concerns or ideas and please visit our WiSH Education Foundation website that directly supports our schools and students.  See how you can help here . 
 On behalf of the Governing Board and our staff, I thank you for your ongoing support of the William S. Hart Union High School District. 
 Sincerely, 
 Michael Vierra, Ph.D. Superintendent
