Newhall Boy Dies in Vietnam.

The Newhall Signal and Saugus Enterprise | Wednesday, May 7, 1969.

The tragedy of Vietnam death has hit close to home in Valencia Valley again.

Warrant Officer Stephen R. Peterson of Newhall was killed April 23 in a midair helicopter collision during a night raid in Vietnam.

The 21-year-old son of Ray and Margaret Peterson of Newhall was a 1965 graduate of Hart High School.

Graveside services are pending in San Diego.

The young serviceman is also survived by his brother Robert Peterson of Huntington Beach; a stepsister, Linda Sissener of Saugus; grandparents Mr. and Mrs. George Peterson of South Dakota and four nieces.
